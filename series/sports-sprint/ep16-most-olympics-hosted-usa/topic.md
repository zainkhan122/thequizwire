## Why this, why now
Paris 2024 made France a three-time Summer host and London is a three-time city, so most viewers reach for France or Britain.
Counting Summer and Winter together the United States has hosted 8 Games (St. Louis, Los Angeles ×2, Atlanta; Lake Placid ×2,
Squaw Valley, Salt Lake City), France 6, Britain 3 — and LA 2028 and Salt Lake City 2034 are already awarded, so the gap grows.
Five city pins on one map, one counter, and a closing fact (LA becomes the third three-time Summer city) that is forward-looking.
