# Which country has the MOST elephants?

## Hook fact
Botswana: about 130,000 savanna elephants — more than the next two countries put together, in a nation of 2.5 million people.

## Show it
A bar race first: Botswana's bar runs away from Zimbabwe and Tanzania (the 2014–15 Great Elephant Census numbers).
Then two blocks: 130,000 elephants next to 2.5 million people — one elephant for every 19 people. Then the camera flies
north to the Okavango Delta with an elephant sticker and the pin: a wetland in the middle of a desert is where they all are.

## Beat order
1. Bars (how far ahead) → 2. Compare blocks elephants vs people (the human-scale surprise) → 3. Okavango flyover + pin +
sticker (the place). Number → ratio → place.

## Different from
Wild #10 (Komodo) is counter → pin hop → venom flash. Here: bar race → compare blocks → one flyover with a sticker; no counter,
no flash, no character beat.

## Swipe test
8 s: Botswana lifts off the map with the flag; 130,000 banner.
15 s: three bars racing, Botswana's still growing.
23 s: a tiny block beside a giant one — elephants vs people — then the camera dives to the delta.

## Devices
bars (country race), compare (elephants vs people), pin, label, sticker (elephant), cam flyover
