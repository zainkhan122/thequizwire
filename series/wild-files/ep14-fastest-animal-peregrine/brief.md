# Brief — Wild Files #14 · Which animal is the FASTEST on Earth?

## Hook fact
The peregrine falcon's hunting dive has been recorded at 389 km/h — the fastest speed of any animal — more than three times a
cheetah (~112 km/h) or a car at motorway speed (110 km/h). The record was measured over Washington State, USA.

## Show it
Wide-drift opener over the western Atlantic under the question; three emoji options (no flags — the answer is an animal, so the
United States is lit only as the place of the record). Reveal = a flash cut, the US fills with a pulse and the 389 KM/H card
lands. Hold on the US while three compare blocks rise — falcon dive, cheetah, motorway car — the speeds are seen side by side,
cheetah and car stickers land on their words, a flash and a pulse on "triples". Push into the Pacific Northwest: the Washington
State tag, and on "Frightful" the falcon sticker RISES out of the United States like a pop-up; a parachute sticker on
"skydiver". Pull out across the Atlantic for the comeback: a skull sticker on "pesticides" swaps for a cityscape on
"skyscrapers", New York pin and tag, then the London pin.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — flash-cut, US fill-pulse, 389 KM/H card.

4. s07 comeback — ☠️ on "pesticides" → 🏙 on "skyscrapers"; New York pin + tag on "York", London pin on "London".

## Different from
#13 (lions) was a counter and an erase; #12 (polar bears) drew the equator and pinned both poles; #11 (elephants) was bars.
This one is the first Wild Files compare chart, the first use of geo.rise in the series (a sticker popping out of the map), and
the first flash-cut reveal in Wild Files — show-set {compare, rise, flash} appears nowhere in the last 12 episodes.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — the Atlantic drifting under FASTEST in yellow; three animal emoji about to land.
- 7 s — options up, timer running; most viewers have said cheetah.
- 14 s — the falcon block towers over the cheetah and the car — the picture that makes the answer land.
- 22 s — a falcon pops up out of the map where the record was set.
