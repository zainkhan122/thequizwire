## Why this, why now
Everyone picks Italy (espresso) or the United States (the bottomless cup). By weight of beans Finland leads at about 12 kg
per person a year — roughly four cups a day (ICO; ThisisFINLAND). The body payoff: four cups is about 400 mg of caffeine,
exactly the daily amount EFSA and the FDA call safe for healthy adults. The cultural kicker: two coffee breaks a day are
written into Finnish collective labour agreements.
