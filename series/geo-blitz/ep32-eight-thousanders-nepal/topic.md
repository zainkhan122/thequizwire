# How many peaks above 8000 m stand in NEPAL?

## Signal
suggest: "how many 8000m peaks are in nepal" (YouTube autocomplete under "eight thousanders", checked 2026-11-17);
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eight-thousander; our stats (dispatch/stats.md): Geo Blitz how-many questions
(#10 Nile) keep comments open longest because viewers argue the number; the channel has not done a mountain count.

## Why this, why now
Everyone knows Everest is in Nepal; almost nobody knows the country holds or shares eight of the fourteen
eight-thousanders (Nepal Department of Tourism; Britannica), while Pakistan and China have five each. The twist is
where they stand: most on the China or India line, only a few entirely inside Nepal; the tallest was measured to
the metre by both countries together in 2020. A number question lets the map show Nepal from the start.

## Viewer takeaway
"Nepal has eight of the fourteen 8,000 m peaks, more than any country; Pakistan and China have five each; most of
Nepal's stand on its borders; Everest is 8,849 m, agreed by Nepal and China in 2020."

## Question shape
how-many, a count with a too-low trap ("how many peaks above 8000 m stand in nepal?"); number options; reads
verbatim as the hook; hook = challenge; wide-drift opener along the range (the answer is a number, so the country may
be in frame).

## Options and why they fool
A 5 — the Pakistan figure, and what people guess for a small country · B 8 (answer) — more than half of all
eight-thousanders · C 11 — the overshoot for people who think "all of them".

## Search term
"peaks in nepal"

## Rejected alternatives
- "Which country has the most 8,000 m peaks" — repeats the which-country-most stem of the last run.
- "How tall is Everest" — a single number, no map story.
- "Which is the second-highest mountain" — K2 is a well-worn answer.
