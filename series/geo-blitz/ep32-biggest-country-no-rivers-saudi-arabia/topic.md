## Why this, why now
"A country with no rivers" is a classic trivia line that usually stops at the name. Saudi Arabia, about 2.15 million
km², has no permanent river and no natural lake (Britannica); its water comes from brief wadi floods, old aquifers and,
above all, the sea — it is the world's largest desalination producer, and the capital of millions drinks water piped
hundreds of kilometres inland from the Gulf. The pipeline is the picture: a route the viewer can watch being drawn.
