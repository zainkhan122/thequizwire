## Why this, why now
After 2004 most people file tsunamis under the Indian Ocean. About 7 in 10 recorded tsunamis start in the Pacific, along the
Ring of Fire; the deadliest one, though, was the 2004 Indian Ocean wave (~230,000 lives). Japan is hit more often than anywhere
and gave the world the word (harbour wave). The takeaway is the UN day's one rule: long quake, high ground. An ocean answer drawn
as a ring, not a country fill — different from #20–#22, which were all country reveals.
