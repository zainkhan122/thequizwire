# Brief — Body & Brain #17 · Where do the MOST people aged 100 or more live?

## Hook fact
Japan has the most centenarians of any country: 99,763 people aged 100+ as of 1 September 2025, a record for the 55th
straight year, about 88% of them women; the first survey in 1963 counted 153 (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare;
NHK). That is roughly 80 per 100,000 people. The United States counted about 80,000 centenarians in the 2020 census,
roughly 24–30 per 100,000 (US Census Bureau); China is estimated at roughly 50,000–60,000, under 5 per 100,000, despite
ten times Japan's population (UN World Population Prospects 2024). Okinawa is known for longevity (Britannica). Since
1963 the government has given each new centenarian a silver sake cup; in 2016 it switched to silver-plated because of
the cost of rising numbers (The Japan Times).
