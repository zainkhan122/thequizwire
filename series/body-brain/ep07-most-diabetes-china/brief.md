# Brief — Body & Brain #7 · In which country do MOST people with diabetes live?

## Hook fact
China has about 148 million adults living with diabetes — roughly 1 in 4 of the 589 million worldwide (IDF Diabetes Atlas 2025).
India is second with about 90 million; the United States has about 38 million.

## Show it
Wide-drift opener over the Indian Ocean under the question, three flags over their countries. Reveal = China lifts into red
with a pulse, "China" tag, the Beijing pin drops on "million" and the 1 IN 4 card lands. Pull back so China and India share the
frame: India lights dim with "India · 2nd" (and stays lit until the outro clear — no erase, the gate found erase in 5 of the last 8) and three compare blocks rise — 148 M, 90 M, 38 M — the gap is seen, not read; the US
flag-ball shrugs on "States". Pull right out to the world: a counter climbs to 589 million adults worldwide, a globe sticker on
"billion", a question-mark sticker and a pulse on "know" for the undiagnosed half. Then the camera swings across the Atlantic to
Toronto: the 14 NOV card on "November", the Toronto pin and "insulin, 1921" tag on "birthday", a syringe sticker on "insulin".

## Different from
#6 (stroke deaths) was a counter then a card; #5 (heart transplant) had a day counter and a rising photo; #4 (tallest men) used a
then-vs-now compare. This one puts a three-country compare first, then a world counter, then jumps continents for a date hook —
show-set {compare, counter} with a Toronto ending that no other episode has, and the reveal is a colour lift, not a fill.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — the Indian Ocean drifting under MOST in yellow; three flags about to land.
- 7 s — flags up, timer running; most viewers have said the United States.
- 14 s — three blocks rise and the first is nearly four times the third — the picture that makes the answer land.
- 25 s — the camera swings across the Atlantic to a pin in Canada while the voice names the day.
