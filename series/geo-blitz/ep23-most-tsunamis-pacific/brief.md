# Brief — Geo Blitz #23 · Which ocean produces the MOST tsunamis?

## Hook fact
About 70 % of recorded tsunamis occur in the Pacific Ocean, generated along the Ring of Fire (NOAA NCEI; ITIC). The deadliest on
record was the 26 December 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami, about 230,000 lives.

## Show it
Wide-drift opener over the whole globe under the question; three wave-emoji options (oceans have no flags). Reveal = no country
fill: the Ring of Fire draws itself around the Pacific in two arcs as the voice says "Pacific", the tag "Pacific · Ring of Fire"
and the 7 IN 10 card. Swing west to the Indian Ocean for the twist: tag, the Banda Aceh pin, and a counter that climbs slowly to
230,000 with a candle sticker — the tone is tender, the pause long. Push onto Japan: the Sendai pin, then a JAPANESE / EVERYWHERE
split — TSU · NAMI, harbour · wave, on the left; 津波 and "the word the world uses" on the right; a wave sticker. Pull back over the
Pacific: the 5 NOV card, a siren sticker on "lesson", a mountain sticker on "ground".

## Different from
#22 (oil) was bars + a drawn belt + a holds/pumps split; #21 bars + a canoe; #20 counters + a city split. This one has no
country highlight at all — the reveal is a route drawn around an ocean — and the show-set {route, counter, split} with a
tender-tone toll beat is unlike any of the last 12; the split is a word, not a number.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — the whole globe drifting under MOST in yellow; three wave emoji about to land.
- 7 s — options up, timer running; most viewers have said Indian.
- 12 s — a line draws itself all the way around the Pacific — the picture that makes the answer land.
- 19 s — a counter climbs past 200,000 beside a candle while the voice drops to a tender tone.
