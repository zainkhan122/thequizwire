# Brief — Body & Brain #13 · Which country drinks the MOST coffee per person?

## Hook fact
Finland consumes about 12 kg of coffee per person per year, the most in the world; Italy about 6 kg, the United States about
4.7 kg (ICO; Wikipedia list). 12 kg is roughly four cups a day. EFSA (2015) and the FDA put the safe habitual intake for
healthy adults at 400 mg of caffeine a day — about four cups of filter coffee. Most Finnish collective labour agreements
guarantee two coffee breaks (kahvitauko) a working day; long dark winters are the usual explanation (ThisisFINLAND).
