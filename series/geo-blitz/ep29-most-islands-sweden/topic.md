# Which country has the MOST islands?

## Signal

Venezuela) are the series' best hold-through-reveal shape.

## Why this, why now
Everyone says Indonesia or the Philippines; the answer is Sweden with about 267,000 islands (Statistics Sweden), more than
the two tropical archipelagos put together. Only about 1,000 are inhabited, Stockholm sits on 14, and the land is still
rising since the ice age so new islands keep appearing — a bars-then-counter-then-map story with a real surprise at every
step.

## Viewer takeaway
"Sweden has the most islands — about 267,000, more than Indonesia (17,000) and the Philippines (7,600) together — and only
about 1,000 are inhabited."
