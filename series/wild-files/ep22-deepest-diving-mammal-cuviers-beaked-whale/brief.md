# Brief — Wild Files #22 · Which mammal dives the DEEPEST?

## Hook fact
Cuvier's beaked whale holds both mammal diving records: 2,992 m, logged by satellite tag off Southern California
(Schorr et al., PLoS ONE 2014), and 222 minutes — 3 h 42 min — on one breath off Cape Hatteras, North Carolina (Quick et al.,
J. Exp. Biol. 2020; NOAA Fisheries). Sperm whales are recorded to about 2,250 m and southern elephant seals to about
2,388 m (Wikipedia). The whale survives because its lungs collapse, its heart slows and its muscles store the oxygen, under
roughly 300 atmospheres at 3,000 m (Ocean Conservation Research; NOAA).
