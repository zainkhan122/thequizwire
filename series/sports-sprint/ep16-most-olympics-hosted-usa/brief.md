# Brief — Sports Sprint #16 · Which country has HOSTED the most Olympic Games?

## Hook fact
Not France (6) or Britain (3): the United States has hosted 8 Olympic Games — St. Louis 1904, Los Angeles 1932 and 1984, Atlanta 1996, Lake Placid 1932 and 1980, Squaw Valley 1960, Salt Lake City 2002 — and LA 2028 plus Salt Lake City 2034 make it 10.

## Show it
A North-Atlantic Mercator stage holding all three countries at k 0.5 under the question (split-candidates opener, nothing readable before the reveal). Reveal = the United States lifts in Olympic blue, "United States · 8" tag, 8 GAMES card, and the city pins drop as the voice names them (St. Louis, Salt Lake City, LA on "summer", Lake Placid on "winter"). Then the camera pulls back across the Atlantic: France lights with "France · 6" and its flag sticker, Britain with "United Kingdom · 3" and its flag, the US pulses. Back onto the US: the LA and Salt Lake pins re-drop and the counter runs 8 → 10 with the happy flag-ball. Wide again for the 3-TIME CITIES card, London and Paris flag stickers, the medal.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — colour-lift US, tag, 8 GAMES card, pins ×4 on cue words.
2. s05 compare — France · 6 / United Kingdom · 3 tags + flag stickers; US pulse.
3. s06 future — LA and Salt Lake pins re-drop; counter 8 → 10 "US Games by 2034"; 🏅 sticker; happy flag-ball.

## Different from
Sports Sprint #15 (Davis Cup) was a Boston pin + photo-rise history piece; #14 (cricket) a bars chart. This one is a five-pin host map with a forward-running counter and no photo; the last beat is about the future, not the past.

## Swipe test

- 6 s — three flags up, timer running; most viewers have said "France".
- 14 s — four city pins drop in two seconds while the voice says "4 summer and 4 winter" — the moment the Winter Games get counted.
