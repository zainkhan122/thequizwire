# Brief — Sports Sprint #18 · Who has scored the MOST World Cup goals ever?

## Hook fact
Kylian Mbappé, 22 World Cup goals after the 2026 tournament (AP; ESPN) — passed Messi's 21 with two goals in the third-place
game v England in Miami Gardens, 18 July 2026, a match France lost 6–4. Klose's 16 had stood since 2014.

## Show it
Wide-drift opener over the mid-Atlantic at k 0.5 so neither France nor the Americas reads as the answer; three flag options
(de, ar, fr) for three men. Reveal = flag-lift on France with the tag "France · 22" and the 22 GOALS card. Pull back over the
Atlantic for the table: a four-row bars chart (Mbappé 22 in red, Messi 21, Klose 16, Ronaldo 15) building row by row, the
France highlight pulsing on "Ronaldo" and "went", a football sticker on "went". Fly to Miami: the Hard Rock Stadium pin with a
flash, the date tag, a counter ticking 0 → 10 (his tournament total) on "brace", a bronze-medal sticker on "lost". Back out
wide for the clock: a KLOSE / MESSI split — 12 YEARS on the left, 1 MONTH on the right — an hourglass sticker and a sad
Argentine flag-ball on "June"; tender tone, wry line.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — flag-lift France, tag, 22 GOALS card.
2. s05 the table — chart.bars_in ×4 rows, highlight_pulse ×2, ⚽ sticker + pulse.
3. s06 the night — pin + fx.flash Miami, date tag, chart.counter_run 0 → 10, 🥉 sticker + pulse.
4. s07 the clock — chart.split_in 12 YEARS / 1 MONTH, ⏳ sticker + pulse, sad flag-ball.

## Different from
#17 (Connolly) was a compare chart + a stadium pin + a bus sticker; #16 counters; #15 a route to Boston; #14 bars.
This one crosses an ocean mid-explanation (Europe → Miami → wide), ends on a time split rather than a number, and its
reveal is a person's country rather than the answer itself — none of the last 12 Sports episodes did that.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — open ocean drifting under MOST in yellow, three flags about to land, no land mass gives it away.
- 7 s — options up, timer running; most viewers have said Klose or Messi.
- 12 s — France lifts under the tricolore; the 22 GOALS card lands.
- 18 s — four bars grow and the red one on top is not Messi's.
