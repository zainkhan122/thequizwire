## Why this, why now
Everyone guesses the United States (famous heart-disease problem) or India (biggest population). It is China: about 2.6 million
stroke deaths in 2021, one in three of the 7.3 million worldwide, sixteen times the US figure of 165,393. A health fact that is still
a map story — three giants, one answer, two live counters — and the FAST card at the end is the thing a viewer actually keeps.
Shipped for 29 October, when the topic is in feeds and the hashtag is live.

## Viewer takeaway
"A third of all stroke deaths on Earth happen in one country — and FAST (face, arm, speech, time) is how you spot one."

which-country, superlative ("records the MOST") so the stem is new for the series; reads verbatim as the hook, lands on the map

## Options and why they fool
A United States — the country people associate with heart disease and strokes in the news · B India — the most populous country,
so "most deaths" feels automatic · C China (answer) — ageing population, high blood pressure and salt-heavy diets make stroke
the leading cause of death there; people rarely connect China with stroke at all.
