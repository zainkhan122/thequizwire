# Brief — Geo Blitz #26 · Which country has the LONGEST coastline?

## Hook fact
Canada's coastline is 202,080 km on the Pacific, Arctic and Atlantic — the world's longest; Indonesia 54,716 km, Russia
37,653 km (CIA World Factbook). Canada has 52,455 islands, most of its coast Arctic and ice-bound for much of the year
(Statistics Canada; Britannica). At 20 km a day the walk would take nearly 28 years.

## Show it
Night-lights opener over the dark north under the question; three flag options (ru, id, ca). Reveal = flag-lift on Canada
with the "Canada · 202,080 km" tag and the 202,080 KM card; a 1,000 km scalebar sits bottom-left because distances are
spoken. Ease back so the whole country sits in frame: three coastline columns rise on "Indonesia" with Canada towering,
an island sticker, the Russian flag-ball thinks on "Russia", Canada pulses on "widest". Drift north over the archipelago: a
snowflake on "Arctic", the island counter runs 0 → 52,455 on "islands", an ice-cube sticker and the "Arctic coast ·
ice-bound" tag on "ice". Pull back to the whole country: a walking sticker on "walk", the 28 YEARS card on "years", the
Canadian flag-ball grins, Canada pulses on "nobody".
