# Brief — Sports Sprint #21 · Which track hosted F1's FIRST World Championship race?

## Hook fact
The first Formula One World Championship race was the British Grand Prix at Silverstone on 13 May 1950, on a circuit laid
out over the runways and perimeter track of RAF Silverstone, a wartime Wellington bomber base (Silverstone Circuit;
Formula1.com). King George VI and Queen Elizabeth attended; Giuseppe Farina won in an Alfa Romeo 158 and clinched the
first drivers' title at Monza on 3 September. That season had 7 rounds; the 2026 calendar has 24.
