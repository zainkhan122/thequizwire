# Brief — Wild Files #24 · Which animal makes the LONGEST migration?

## Hook fact
The Arctic tern (Sterna paradisaea) makes the longest known animal migration. Geolocators on birds from Greenland and
Iceland recorded a mean annual round trip of about 70,900 km (up to 81,600 km) on an S-shaped route down the Atlantic
to the Weddell Sea and back (Egevang et al., PNAS 2010). It breeds in the Arctic summer and winters at the Antarctic
ice edge, so it sees two summers a year and more daylight than any other animal; over a roughly 30-year life it covers
about 2.4 million km, about three return trips to the Moon (Audubon; Britannica; NASA Earth–Moon distance). It weighs
about 86–127 g (Cornell). Humpback whales migrate up to about 8,000 km each way (NOAA); eastern monarchs up to about
4,800 km (USFWS).
