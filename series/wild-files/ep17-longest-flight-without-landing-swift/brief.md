# Brief — Wild Files #17 · Which bird flies the LONGEST without landing?

## Hook fact
Micro data loggers fitted to common swifts breeding in southern Sweden showed the birds airborne for the whole ten-month
non-breeding period; three individuals never landed once (Hedenström et al., Current Biology 2016; Lund University). They
eat aerial insects, drink by skimming water and sleep in short bouts during slow glides at altitude; they are on land for
roughly two months a year to nest; fledglings may fly two to three years before first landing; they winter over the Congo
basin, tracking rain fronts (BTO; RSPB; Britannica).
