## Why this, why now
With the 2026 Winter Games fresh in the feed, the origin question has a built-in audience and a wrong reflex answer:
Switzerland (the ski-resort country, host of the second Games) or Norway (the medal superpower). The first Winter
Olympics were at Chamonix, France, in 1924 — staged as an "International Winter Sports Week" and only named the first
Winter Games by the IOC in 1926 (olympics.com; Britannica). 16 nations and 258 athletes came; Norway took 17 medals and
the host went home with three bronzes. A who-was-first stem with a host-that-did-not-win twist.

## Viewer takeaway
