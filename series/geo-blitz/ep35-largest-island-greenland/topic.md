## Why this, why now
People reach for Australia (a continent) or for the tropical giants they can picture — Borneo, Madagascar. Greenland is
about 2.17 million km², almost 3 times Borneo (748,000) or New Guinea, and nearly 4 times Madagascar (587,000)
(Britannica). About 80 percent of it lies under a single ice sheet up to 3 km thick (NSIDC), and the whole island holds
around 56,000 people, most on the south-west coast around Nuuk (Statistics Greenland). A size nobody has a feel for,
told as three islands you can see, then one you can stand on.
