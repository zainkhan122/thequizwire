# Brief — Body & Brain #9 · Which country gave the world its FIRST vaccine?

## Hook fact
Edward Jenner, Berkeley, Gloucestershire, England, 14 May 1796: cowpox from milkmaid Sarah Nelmes's hand into 8-year-old
James Phipps; a later smallpox challenge had no effect (Riedel 2005; Britannica). Smallpox killed about 30 % of those infected
and was declared eradicated by the WHO in 1980 (WHO).
