# Brief — Geo Blitz #32 · Which is the BIGGEST country with no rivers?

## Hook fact
Saudi Arabia has no permanent rivers or lakes; drainage is through wadis that carry water only after rain (Britannica,
"Drainage and soils"); area 2,149,690 km² (CIA Factbook). It is the world's largest producer of desalinated water, about
a fifth of global output, and desalination supplies more than half of its drinking water (SWCC; Britannica
"Desalination"). Riyadh sits on the Najd plateau at about 600 m, roughly 400 km from the Gulf, and is supplied by
desalinated water piped from Al-Jubayl (Britannica "Riyadh", "Al-Jubayl"). Australia's Murray–Darling is permanent;
Egypt's people live along the Nile.
