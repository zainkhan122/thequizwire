# Brief — Geo Blitz #24 · Which country has the MOST time zones?

## Hook fact
France uses 12 time zones — metropolitan France plus overseas territories from Tahiti (UTC−10) to Wallis and Futuna (UTC+12),
22 hours apart (timeanddate). Russia 11 across one landmass; the USA 11 including territories; China one official zone (UTC+8).

## Show it
Mystery-zoom-out opener from the Indian Ocean under the question; three flag options (ru, us, fr). Reveal = fill-pulse on
France with the "France · 12 zones" tag and the 12 ZONES card. Pull back over Europe and Russia for the table — three bars,
France on top of two elevens — the Russian flag-ball slumps on "Russia", a clock sticker on "France". Swing across the Pacific:
the Tahiti pin on "Tahiti", the Wallis pin on "Wallis", a TAHITI / WALLIS split with the two offsets, a sun sticker that
becomes a moon on "gap" — dawn and dusk in one country. Fly west to China: it lights in the dim second colour on "China", the
1 CLOCK card and "China · 1 zone" tag on "single", an alarm-clock sticker pulsing on "everywhere".

episode and ends on a second-colour highlight of a different country — the first Geo Blitz split that compares clock offsets.
