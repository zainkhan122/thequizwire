# Brief — Body & Brain #12 · In which city was insulin FIRST injected into a patient?

## Hook fact
Insulin was first injected into a patient at Toronto General Hospital on 11 January 1922 — Leonard Thompson, 14, weighing
29 kg. The first extract was impure and produced a sterile abscess; James Collip's purified extract, given from 23 January,
dropped his blood sugar from 0.52 % to 0.12 % (Banting, Best, Collip et al., CMAJ 1922; U of T Insulin 100). Banting and
Best had isolated it from dog pancreases in Macleod's lab in the summer of 1921; the patent was assigned to the University
of Toronto for $1.
