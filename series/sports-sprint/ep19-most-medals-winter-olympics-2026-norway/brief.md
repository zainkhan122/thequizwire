# Brief — Sports Sprint #19 · Which country topped the Milan Cortina Winter Games medal table?

## Hook fact
Norway topped the Milano Cortina 2026 table with 18 golds — the most by any nation at a single Winter Games — and 41 medals;
the USA 12 golds / 33 (its best ever); hosts Italy 10 / 30; Johannes Klæbo 6 golds (Olympics.com; USA Today; Outlook).

## Show it
Split-candidates opener: the USA, Italy and Norway each get a glance under the question; three flag options (us, it, no).
Reveal = colour-lift on Norway with the "Norway · 18 golds" tag and the 18 GOLDS card. Ease back over the North Sea for the
golds: three compare columns — Norway 18 (41 total), USA 12 (33), Italy 10 (30) — the US flag-ball thinking on "States", a
gold-medal sticker on "Norway". Dive onto northern Italy: the Milan pin, then Cortina, and on "skier" a KLAEBO / CANADA
split — 6 golds, one man, against 5 golds, one nation — with a skier sticker. Ease back so all of Norway fills the frame: the < 6 MILLION card, then on "beat" a slumped US flag-ball and a 340 MILLION card
stacked above it, a snowflake on "snow" and a grinning Norwegian flag-ball.

## Different from
#18 (World Cup goals) was bars + a stadium pin + a 12-years/1-month split; #17 a compare + route + stadium pin; #16 counters.
This one closes on two stacked population cards and a slumped runner-up flag-ball instead of a map device, pins two host
cities in one beat, and its split compares a person with a country — none of the last 12 Sports episodes did that.
