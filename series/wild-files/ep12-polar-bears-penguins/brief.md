# Brief — Wild Files #12 · Polar bears and penguins have NEVER met in the wild. True or false?

## Hook fact
True. Polar bears are Arctic-only (Svalbard, Churchill, Alaska's North Slope); every penguin lives south of the equator or, in the Galápagos, right on it. Every picture of the two together was staged or drawn.

## Show it
The whole world on one Mercator stage, drifting under the question. Reveal = the equator draws itself across the globe (route-draw), bear pins pop above it (Svalbard, Churchill, Utqiaġvik), penguin pins below it (Antarctic Peninsula, Boulders Beach, Phillip Island), two tag labels name the halves, OPPOSITE POLES card. Then the camera slides to the eastern Pacific and the Galápagos pin lands exactly on the line; swings to Alaska (Christmas tree ✕, "bears, no penguins" tag, polar-bear sticker); and ends wide with two real photographs sliding in — polar bears on Arctic ice from the left, Adélie penguins at Cape Royds from the right.

1. s04 reveal — equator route-draw, six pins in two waves, two half-world tags, fact card.

## Different from
Wild Files #10 (Komodo) used five island pins + venom card; #11 (elephants) a counter and a country fill. This is the first true/false on the channel, the first reveal with no country highlight at all (a drawn line + two pin sets), no flag-ball characters, and the first two-photo ending.
