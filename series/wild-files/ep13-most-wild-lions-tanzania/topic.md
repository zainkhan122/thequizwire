# Where do MOST of the world's wild lions live?

## Signal
suggest: "animal facts shorts", "which country has the most …" are live stems; our stats
(dispatch/stats.md): Wild Files is the series that has waited longest in the rotation and its country-superlative episodes
(tigers India, elephants Botswana) are the two best Wild Files performers — the format works, the species changes.

## Why this, why now
Everyone names Kenya (the Maasai Mara) or South Africa (Kruger). Tanzania's 2024/25 national census found about 17,200 lions —
more than every other country combined, against an African total of only 22–25 thousand. Lions are gone from 94 % of their
historic range, and the only wild lions outside Africa are 891 Asiatic lions in Gir, India. Three verifiable numbers, one map,
and a last beat that flies to a country nobody expects in a lion story.

## Viewer takeaway
"Most of the world's lions live in one country, there are fewer than 25,000 left in Africa, and India has the only others."

## Question shape
where-is ("where do MOST … live"), a place question rather than the series' usual "which country has" — same stage as the tigers/elephants episodes but a different continent spread
(East Africa) and a different ending (India). Reads verbatim as the hook; no numbers in the question.

## Options and why they fool
A Kenya — the safari brand everyone knows, home of the Mara crossing footage · B South Africa — Kruger, the Big Five tourism machine
· C Tanzania (answer) — the Serengeti and Selous/Nyerere hold most of Africa's lions, but people file the country under
"Kilimanjaro", not lions.

- "Which country has the most tigers" — already Wild Files #8.
- "How many lions are left in the world" — a number, not a map; kept as the second beat instead.
