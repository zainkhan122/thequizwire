# Brief — Sports Sprint #23 · Which country has the MOST Olympic golds in men's field hockey?

## Hook fact
India has 8 Olympic golds in men's field hockey (1928, 1932, 1936, 1948, 1952, 1956, 1964, 1980), the most of any
nation; Germany 4, Pakistan 3, Great Britain 3, Netherlands 2, Australia 1 (Olympedia; Wikipedia medal table). Six of
the golds were consecutive, Amsterdam 1928 to Melbourne 1956, without losing a match (IOC; FIH). India beat the United
States 24–1 at Los Angeles 1932, the biggest win in Olympic hockey (IOC results). Dhyan Chand, "the Wizard", led the
pre-war sides and lived in Jhansi (Britannica). The last gold was Moscow 1980; bronze followed at Tokyo 2020 and Paris
2024 (Wikipedia).
