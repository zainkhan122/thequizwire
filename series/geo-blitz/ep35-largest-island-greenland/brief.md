# Brief — Geo Blitz #35 · Which is the LARGEST island on Earth?

## Hook fact
Greenland is the largest island in the world at about 2,166,086 km² (Britannica; Statistics Greenland). New Guinea
is second at about 786,000 km², Borneo third at about 748,000 km² and Madagascar fourth at about 587,000 km² — bigger
than metropolitan France (Britannica). Australia, at 7.7 million km², is classed as a continent. About 80 percent of
Greenland lies under the Greenland Ice Sheet, up to about 3 km thick (NSIDC). The population is about 56,000, most of
it on the ice-free south-west coast; the capital Nuuk has about 20,000 (Statistics Greenland; CIA World Factbook).
