# Which of these joined the United Nations MOST recently?

## Signal
calendar: United Nations Day, 24 October (config/calendar.json) — UN explainers trend every year that week; suggest: YouTube
autocomplete "how many countries are in the un" / "newest country in the world" (2026-10-21, review/signals.md).

## Why this, why now
UN Day gives a three-day window where "UN" is in feeds. The surprising part is not the newest member but who the late joiners
were: Switzerland only joined in 2002 (most people assume it was a founder) — that is the trap, and the explanation can show
all three join dates on one map with the newest country drawn as it was born in 2011.

## Viewer takeaway
"Switzerland wasn't in the UN until 2002 — and the newest member is a country younger than Instagram."

## Question shape
which-most-recent (ordering question) — three real members, three real dates; the viewer is ordering facts they half-know.
Not a superlative stem ("which country most" is burned for a month) and not a yes/no.

## Options and why they fool
A Switzerland — famous for neutrality, "surely a founder" (joined 2002, by referendum) · B South Sudan (answer, 2011) ·
C Montenegro — recent independence (2006) makes it a credible newest.

## Search term
"newest country in the world"

## Rejected alternatives
- "How many countries are in the UN: 193 / 195 / 206" — good search term, but the answer is a number, not a place; no map reveal, and the 193-vs-195 debate (Vatican, Palestine observers) muddies the fact check.
- "Where is the UN headquartered" — too easy; swipe at second 2.

Instagram adds #geography #mapquiz; FB asks "did you know Switzerland joined so late?" is a spoiler? No — Switzerland is a
distractor, not the answer, so it can be named; South Sudan and "2011" cannot.
