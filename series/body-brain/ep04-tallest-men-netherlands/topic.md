# Which country has the TALLEST people on average?

## Signal
suggest: YouTube autocomplete for "tallest people" → "tallest people in the world", "tallest people in the world comparison",
"tallest people south sudan" (seen 2026-10-21, review/signals.md). People are searching for a *comparison* and half of them
expect an African country — that is a quiz with a built-in wrong answer.

## Why this, why now
Evergreen search demand with a misconception baked in (South Sudan / Dinka folklore vs the measured national average).
The Netherlands answer surprises most non-Europeans, and the "they stopped growing in the 1990s" twist gives the explanation a
second surprise, which is what keeps a viewer past the reveal. No seasonal hook; this is a demand-driven pick, not a date pick.

## Viewer takeaway
"The Dutch are the tallest on Earth — and Americans used to be, until they fell to 37th."

## Question shape
which-of-three — the misconception needs a visible wrong option (USA = the old champion, Sweden = the 'tall Scandinavian' guess).
A true/false shape would waste the two good distractors.

## Options and why they fool
A Netherlands (answer) · B USA — the tallest nation in 1914, most viewers' default; the fall to 37th is the explanation's payoff ·
C Sweden — the Scandinavian stereotype; actually ~181 cm, a close second-tier, so it is a fair trap not a joke option.

## Search term
"tallest people in the world"

## Rejected alternatives
- "Which country has the most doctors" (also in the suggest list) — strong search term, but the answer (Cuba/Austria depending on source) is contested across publishers → fails the two-publisher ledger rule.
- "Which country has the shortest people" — real search demand, but it humiliates a nation on a reach-driven channel; not our tone.
