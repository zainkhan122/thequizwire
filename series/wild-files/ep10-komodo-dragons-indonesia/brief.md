# Where do KOMODO DRAGONS live in the wild?

## Hook fact
Only Indonesia — a handful of islands in the Lesser Sundas — and there are only about 3,400 of them left in the wild.

## Show it
After the reveal the camera stays on the islands and a counter climbs 0 → 3,458 under "left in the wild": the whole species
on one screen. Then an island hop: the camera slides along the chain and pins drop Komodo → Rinca → Flores one by one,
with the national park label — the entire range in three taps. Last, the sting: a "VENOM" card with a flash, and the Indonesia
flag-ball pops in with a wow face — the fact most people still get wrong (it is venom, not bacteria, proven 2009).

## Beat order
1. Counter (how few) → 2. Pin hop (how small the range is) → 3. Venom card + flash + character (the surprise). Rare → tiny
→ dangerous.

## Different from
Wild #11 (Botswana elephants) is a bar race against other countries then a people-vs-elephants comparison and a wetland flyover.
Here there is no chart comparison at all: counter → pin hop → card with a flash.

## Swipe test
8 s: the islands light up and the Komodo pin drops.
15 s: the counter is climbing to 3,458 — "that's all of them?"
23 s: pins hopping down the island chain, camera gliding with them.

## Devices
counter, pin, label, card, flash, character, cam
