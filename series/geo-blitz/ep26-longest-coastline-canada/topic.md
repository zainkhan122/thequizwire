## Why this, why now
The size trap: Russia is the biggest country but its coast is about 37,653 km; Indonesia's 17,000 islands give it 54,716 km;
Canada's Arctic archipelago — 52,455 islands — pushes it to 202,080 km, nearly four times the runner-up (CIA World
Factbook; Statistics Canada). The closer makes it human: at 20 km a day the walk takes nearly 28 years. A whole-country
reveal over three oceans, which no Geo Blitz since #16 has done.
