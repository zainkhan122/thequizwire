# Brief — Sports Sprint #22 · Which country INVENTED table tennis?

## Hook fact
Table tennis began in Victorian England in the 1880s as an after-dinner parlour game on dining tables — books as a net,
cigar-box lids as bats, champagne corks as balls (Britannica; ITTF; Wikipedia). J. Jaques & Son trademarked "Ping-Pong" in
London in 1901 and sold boxed sets with rules. The ITTF was founded and the first World Championships held in London in
1926; the sport joined the Olympics at Seoul 1988. Through Paris 2024 China has won 37 of 42 golds; Japan 1; Great
Britain 0.
