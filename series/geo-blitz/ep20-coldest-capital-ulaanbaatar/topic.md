## Why this, why now
Everyone reaches for Ottawa or Moscow. Ulaanbaatar is the only national capital whose average temperature across the whole
year is below freezing; Astana is the runner-up, Ottawa is nowhere near. The why is altitude (about 1,350 m on a plateau far
from any ocean) and the kicker is scale: about half of all Mongolians live in that one cold valley. A different shape from #19
(a capital, not a village; a why-beat, not a record) riding the same proven interest within 48 hours.
