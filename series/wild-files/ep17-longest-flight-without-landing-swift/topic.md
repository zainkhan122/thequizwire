# Which bird flies the LONGEST without landing?

## Signal
suggest: "bird that never lands", "which bird can fly the longest" (YouTube autocomplete, checked 2026-11-11); the 2016
Lund result still circulates as a viral fact every autumn when swifts leave Europe. wikipedia:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_swift. our stats (dispatch/stats.md): Wild Files' record questions with a
counter-intuitive winner (#14 fastest animal, #15 longest-living) are the series' best holders; this is the same shape
where the famous options (albatross, tern) lose to the small one.

## Why this, why now
The fact is extraordinary and sourced: micro data loggers on swifts breeding in southern Sweden showed a 10-month aerial
phase, three birds never landing at all (Hedenström et al., Current Biology 2016; Lund University). They feed, drink and
sleep on the wing, are on land only for the ~2-month breeding season, young may fly 2–3 years before first landing, and
they winter over the Congo basin following rain fronts (BTO, RSPB, Britannica). Swifts have just left Europe for
Africa, so the migration line is the season's story. #16 was a herd count with bars; this is a duration with an erase
and a route.

## Viewer takeaway
"The common swift — ten months in the air without landing; it eats, drinks and naps on the wing, lands only to nest, and
winters over the Congo basin."

- "Which bird sleeps while flying?" — frigatebirds too (Rattenborg 2016); not a single answer.
