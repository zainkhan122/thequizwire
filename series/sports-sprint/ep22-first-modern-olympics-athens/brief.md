# Brief — Sports Sprint #22 · Where were the FIRST modern Olympics held?

## Hook fact
The first modern Olympic Games opened in Athens on 6 April 1896 in the Panathenaic Stadium, rebuilt in marble with Georgios
Averoff's money (IOC; Panathenaic Stadium). 241 athletes from 14 nations, all men; women first competed at Paris 1900 (IOC).
The first marathon ran about 40 km from Marathon to the stadium and was won by Spyridon Louis, a water carrier (Britannica).
James Connolly (USA) took the triple jump on day one — the first modern champion; he had left Harvard to come (IOC).
