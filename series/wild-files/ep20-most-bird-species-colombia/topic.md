## Why this, why now
Everyone picks Brazil because of the Amazon; Indonesia's seventeen thousand islands are the clever guess. Colombia
holds the record with about 1,969 species, roughly a fifth of all birds (Colombian Committee of Ornithological Records;
BirdLife). The reason is the twist: not jungle but three Andean ranges whose isolated valleys each breed their own
birds, plus two coasts. New species are still being described, and the country has more hummingbirds than anywhere.
