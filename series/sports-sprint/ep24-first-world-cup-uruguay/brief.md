# Brief — Sports Sprint #24 · Where was the FIRST FIFA World Cup played?

## Hook fact
The first FIFA World Cup was played in Uruguay, 13–30 July 1930, entirely in Montevideo, by 13 teams (FIFA tournament
page; Britannica "World Cup"). Uruguay, Olympic champions of 1924 and 1928, built the Estadio Centenario for it; the
stadium was completed five days after the opening matches (FIFA; Wikipedia). Only four European teams travelled:
France, Belgium and Romania sailed on the SS Conte Verde, which collected Brazil's squad in Rio de Janeiro (Wikipedia
"1930 FIFA World Cup", "SS Conte Verde"). Uruguay beat Argentina 4–2 in the final on 30 July 1930 (FIFA; Wikipedia).
Italy hosted the second edition in 1934; Brazil has five titles (Britannica).
