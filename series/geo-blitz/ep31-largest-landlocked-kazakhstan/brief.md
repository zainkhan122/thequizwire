# Brief — Geo Blitz #31 · Which is the LARGEST landlocked country?

## Hook fact
Kazakhstan is the largest landlocked country at 2,724,900 km² (CIA Factbook; ninth-largest country overall, Britannica).
Mongolia is second at about 1,564,000 km², Bolivia about 1,099,000 km². Kazakhstan's western shore is on the Caspian Sea,
the world's largest inland body of water, a lake with no outlet to the ocean (Britannica). Baikonur Cosmodrome in
south-central Kazakhstan is the world's first and largest spaceport, leased to Russia; Sputnik 1 and Gagarin's Vostok 1
launched from it (NASA; Britannica).
