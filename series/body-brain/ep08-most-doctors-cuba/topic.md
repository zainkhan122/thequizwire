## Why this, why now
Everyone guesses Germany (hospitals) or the United States (money). The World Bank/WHO ratio says Cuba, about 9.5 physicians per
1,000 people, double Germany and more than double the US. The twist is that the number at home is falling fast — ONEI counted
106,131 doctors in 2021 and 75,364 in 2024 — while around 37,000 work abroad as the country's main export. A health question
with a geography answer, which is exactly the feed's lane.
