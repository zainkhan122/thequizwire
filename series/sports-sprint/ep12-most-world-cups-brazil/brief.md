# Which country has the MOST World Cup titles?

## Hook fact
Brazil has five stars — and is the only country that has turned up to every single World Cup, all 22 of them.

## Show it
A bar race, not a card: three bars grow — Brazil 5, Germany 4, Italy 4 — Brazil's bar keeps going after the others stop.
Then a counter sprints 1930 → 2022 as "22 of 22" while the camera glides down the coast (attendance, not absence).
Then the twist: the mascot's face flips from happy to sad, the camera dives onto the Maracanã pin and a single card
lands for 1950 with a flash — the one they don't talk about.

## Beat order
1. Bars (the record, instantly readable) → 2. Counter 1930→2022 (the streak nobody knows) → 3. Mascot turns sad, flash,
Maracanã 1950 (emotional payoff). Pride, pride, then a sting — the order a football fan would tell it.

## Different from
Sports #13 (marathon) is a route trace with a runner; this one has no route and no pins until the very last beat —
it is chart → counter → emotional close.

## Swipe test
8 s: the flag lifts over Brazil, flag-balls react; they stay for "how many".
15 s: three bars racing, Brazil's keeps growing; the comparison is the hook.
23 s: the year counter is sprinting while the camera slides down the coast; then the face flips — curiosity spike.

## Devices
bars, counter, character, flash, pin, card (one), cam
