## Why this, why now
Ships inside the World Diabetes Day window. The case file is the hook: on 11 January 1922 at Toronto General Hospital a
14-year-old, Leonard Thompson, got the first insulin injection; the extract was impure and raised an abscess; Collip's
purified extract 12 days later dropped his blood sugar from 0.52 % to 0.12 % (CMAJ 1922; U of T Insulin 100). Banting and
Best had made it from dog pancreases in a borrowed university lab the summer before; the patent went to the university for
$1. #11 was Paris; this moves the series to North America with a city answer and a counter + erase device set.
