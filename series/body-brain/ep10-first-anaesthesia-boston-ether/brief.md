# Brief — Body & Brain #10 · Which country FIRST put a patient to sleep for surgery?

## Hook fact
The first public surgery under anaesthesia: Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston, 16 October 1846 — dentist William Morton
gave ether, surgeon John Collins Warren removed a neck tumour from Gilbert Abbott, who felt nothing (MGH; Linda Hall).
Crawford Long, Jefferson, Georgia, had used ether on 30 March 1842, unpublished (The Conversation; Britannica). Robert
Liston amputated a leg under ether in London on 21 December 1846 (RCS; UCL).
