## Why this, why now
The misconception: Stanford's Norman Shumway developed the operation through the 1960s, so people assume an American
first — but Christiaan Barnard's team of about 30 did it in Cape Town on 3 December 1967, and Stanford followed on 6
January 1968 (Stanford Medicine; CVJA). The human scale: a grocer who lived 18 days, a second patient who lived 19 months,
and today a median of about 12 years (ISHLT). The donor's father consenting that night gives the closer its weight.
