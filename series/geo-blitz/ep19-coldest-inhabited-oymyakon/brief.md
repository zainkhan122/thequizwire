# Brief — Geo Blitz #19 · The COLDEST inhabited place on Earth is in which country?

## Hook fact
Oymyakon, a village of about 500 people in Russia's Sakha Republic, recorded −67.7 °C on 6 February 1933 — the lowest
temperature ever measured where people live — and its school stays open until −52 °C.

## Show it
Night-lights basemap (Black Marble) over northern Eurasia from frame 0, so the whole opener is dark and cold; the camera
drifts east along the Arctic coast under the question. Reveal = Russia fills and pulses, a "SIBERIA" tag lands, the Oymyakon
pin drops on "village" with a "Pole of Cold" tag and the −67.7 °C card. Then a push-in to the village (k 1.9): Yakutsk pins
to the west as the nearest city, the January counter falls 0 → −46 °C. Then the scale: a 3-row bar chart over the dark
interior — your freezer −18, Oymyakon −67.7, Vostok −89.2 — so the viewer feels where the village sits between the kitchen and
Antarctica. Finish on the SCHOOL OPEN · until −52 °C card with the school sticker and the cool Russia flag-ball.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — fill-pulse Russia, SIBERIA tag on "siberia", Oymyakon pin + Pole of Cold tag on "village", −67.7 °C card.
2. s05 village — camera tight on Oymyakon, Yakutsk pin, cold-face sticker on "people", counter 0 → −46 °C on "January".
3. s06 scale — bars freezer / Oymyakon / Vostok grow over the interior; ice-cube on "freezer", Russia pulse on "Antarctica", penguin on "Vostok".
4. s07 school — SCHOOL OPEN card, school sticker, Oymyakon pin pulses back in, cool flag-ball on "older".

## Different from
Ep18 (UN) was three-country second-highlights + a counter + a photo rise; ep17 (Baikal) depth counter + compare + route;
ep16 (border) route + bars. This is the first night-lights opener in Geo Blitz, the first negative counter, and the only
Geo explanation built around a "kitchen-to-Antarctica" scale rather than a comparison of countries.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — the question card lands on a dark night-lights map already drifting; no bare edges, the stage is black and gold, not blue.
- 6 s — options read while the camera keeps sliding east; Russia is NOT highlighted before the reveal.
- 16 s — the January counter is falling past −30 while the frost sticker lands; the freezer bar follows within 3 s.
