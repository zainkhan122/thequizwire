# Which is the LARGEST landlocked country?

## Signal
suggest: "largest landlocked country" (YouTube autocomplete, "largest / smallest / only country that…" family in
review/signals.md, checked 2026-11-13); wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landlocked_country; our stats
(dispatch/stats.md): size-record Geo Blitz questions (#26 coastline, #29 islands) retain best; "landlocked" is the
unused member of that family.

## Why this, why now
Everyone knows Mongolia is landlocked and many guess it is the biggest; Bolivia is the famous "lost its coast" country.
Kazakhstan, at about 2.72 million km², is far bigger than both (CIA Factbook) and has a trap built in: its long western
shore is on the Caspian, which is a lake. The close — the first satellite and the first human in space both launched
from a pad on its steppe — turns a size fact into a story.

## Viewer takeaway
"Kazakhstan is the largest landlocked country (about 2.7 million km²), Mongolia second; the Caspian is a lake, so it
still counts; Baikonur, the world's first spaceport, is inside it."
