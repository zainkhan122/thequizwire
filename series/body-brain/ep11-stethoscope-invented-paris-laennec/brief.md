# Brief — Body & Brain #11 · Where was the stethoscope INVENTED?

## Hook fact
René Laennec invented the stethoscope at the Necker Hospital, Paris, in 1816 — a rolled sheet of paper used to hear a young
woman's heart when pressing an ear to her chest felt improper (Roguin, Clin Med Res 2006; Britannica). He named it from
Greek stethos (chest) + skopein (to look), published 'De l'auscultation médiate' in 1819, and died of tuberculosis in
Quimper in 1826, aged 45.
