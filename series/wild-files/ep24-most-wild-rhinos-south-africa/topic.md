## Why this, why now
Kenya is where the safari photos come from and India has the famous one-horned rhino, so both feel like the answer.
But around 16,000 of the world's roughly 27,000 wild rhinos live in South Africa — about 6 in 10 (International
Rhino Foundation 2024; DFFE). Both African species live there, and the Kruger National Park holds more rhinos than
any other place on Earth (SANParks). A share you can stack as blocks, a species pair you can split, and a park you
can lift off the map.
