# Brief — Geo Blitz #28 · Which river carries the MOST water?

## Hook fact
The Amazon has the greatest discharge of any river, about 209,000 m³/s on average — roughly one fifth of all river water
reaching the oceans (USGS; NASA Earth Observatory; Britannica). The Congo is second at about 41,000 m³/s; the Nile, the
longest river, carries about 2,800–3,000 m³/s (Britannica). The Amazon runs about 6,400 km from the Andes to a mouth more
than 320 km wide. In 1500 Vicente Yáñez Pinzón found fresh water out of sight of land and named it Mar Dulce (Britannica).

a river. Ease back over the basin: three discharge bars rise on "Congo" with the Amazon off the chart, the Egyptian
flag-ball thinks on "Nile", a drop sticker on "barely" as the line re-draws. Hold wide: the length tag and a mountain on
"Andes", the Mouth pin and tag on "mouth", the FLOW 1 IN 5 / MOUTH 320 KM split on "wide". Drift east: a boat rides the line
to the sea, a compass sticker on "captain", the 1500 card on "fresh", the Brazilian flag-ball grins and a cup sticker lands
on "sweet"; the pin leaves at the end.
