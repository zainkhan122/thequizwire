## Why this, why now
Everyone says Russia (widest country) or the USA. France has 12 time zones through its overseas territories — from Tahiti at
UTC−10 to Wallis and Futuna at UTC+12, a 22-hour spread (timeanddate). Russia has 11 across one landmass; the USA 11 counting
its islands. The closer: China, nearly as wide as the USA, uses a single clock. A planet-wide camera journey (Europe → Pacific →
China) that #20–#23 did not do — those were one-region reveals or an ocean ring.

## Viewer takeaway
"France has the most time zones (12, UTC−10 to UTC+12) because of its islands; Russia 11; China uses just one."

## Options and why they fool

"counting territories" hedge · C France (answer) — the hexagon looks like one zone; the islands are the whole trick.
