## Why this, why now
The reflex answers are the medical superpowers: the United States, where the technique was developed on dogs at
Stanford, or Britain. The first human heart transplant was done in Cape Town, South Africa, in the early hours of
Sunday 3 December 1967 by Christiaan Barnard (Britannica; Heart of Cape Town Museum) — a month before the first US adult
transplant. The patient lived 18 days; median survival today is 12+ years, and around 6,000 hearts are transplanted
every year. A country at the far end of the map, a THEN/NOW that is a whole lifetime apart.
