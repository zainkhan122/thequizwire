# Where are MOST of the world's pyramids?

## Signal
suggest: "which country has the most pyramids" (YouTube autocomplete, checked 2026-11-05 next to the "which country has the
most …" family in review/signals.md); wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nubian_pyramids; our stats (dispatch/stats.md):
the Geo Blitz "MOST …" questions with a wrong-obvious answer (#21 lakes, #22 oil, #24 time zones) are the steadiest openers.

## Why this, why now
Everyone answers Egypt. Sudan's Kingdom of Kush built more than 200 pyramids — nearly twice Egypt's ~138 — most of them at
Meroë (UNESCO; National Geographic; Smithsonian). The closer: Kushite kings ruled Egypt itself as pharaohs from 744 BC
(Britannica). A one-river story (Meroë → Khartoum → Giza) that lets the camera travel 1,600 km up the Nile.

## Viewer takeaway
"Sudan has the most pyramids — 200+, built by Kush at Meroë; Egypt has about 138; Kushite kings once ruled Egypt."

## Question shape
which-place, record superlative ("Where are MOST of the world's pyramids?"); flag options; reads verbatim as the hook;
hook = misconception (Egypt is the trap); wide-drift opener over the Red Sea so neither Egypt nor Sudan is favoured.

## Options and why they fool
A Egypt — the word "pyramid" is Egypt in most heads · B Mexico — Teotihuacan and Chichén Itzá give it a real claim ·
C Sudan (answer) — almost nobody pictures Sudan as pyramid country.

## Search term
"which country has the most pyramids"

## Rejected alternatives
- "Which is the tallest pyramid" — Giza; the obvious answer is right.
- "Where is Meroë" — a place answer; better inside the episode as the pin.
- "How many pyramids does Egypt have" — a number answer and the counts vary 118–138 by source.
