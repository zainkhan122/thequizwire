## Why this, why now
Quito is the famous "high capital" and Kathmandu sounds high because of Everest, but La Paz — Bolivia's seat of government
at about 3,640 m — is the highest (Britannica). The twist is that Bolivia has a second, constitutional capital, Sucre, at
2,800 m, and the airport above La Paz sits at 4,060 m. The body close (a third less oxygen; visiting football teams gasp)
makes the number felt.

## Viewer takeaway

capital is Sucre; the air up there has about a third less oxygen."
