# Which country has the TALLEST people on average?

## Hook fact
The Netherlands — Dutch men average about 182.5 cm, 13 cm taller than their great-grandfathers — while American men fell
from 3rd tallest in the world to 37th.

## Show it
Two blocks side by side: 1896 cohort 169 cm vs 1996 cohort 182.5 cm — the century jump as a shape, not a sentence.
Then the US twist as a rank counter rolling 3 → 37 while the camera slides across the North Sea toward Britain and away
from the Netherlands. Then the ending: a "STOPPED" card lands on The Hague with the mascot's think face — the growing is over.

## Beat order
1. Compare blocks then-vs-now (the gain) → 2. Rank counter 3→37 (the fall of the USA) → 3. Stopped card + think face + pin
(the open question). Up → down → flat.

## Different from
This series' other ready episodes are none yet; versus the Geo/Sports set it is the only one that opens with a then-vs-now
compare and ends on a rank counter + think face.

## Swipe test
8 s: the Netherlands lifts off the map, banner 182.5 cm.
15 s: two blocks rising, the second clearly taller — a century of growth.
23 s: the number is spinning down from 3 to 37 while the camera leaves the country — "who dropped?"

## Devices
compare, counter, card, character, pin, cam
