# Brief — Geo Blitz #33 · Where is the WETTEST place on Earth?

## Hook fact
Mawsynram, in the East Khasi Hills of Meghalaya, north-east India, has the highest average annual rainfall on Earth,
about 11,871 mm (Guinness World Records; India Meteorological Department). Manaus in the Amazon receives about 2,300 mm
a year and London about 600 mm (Wikipedia climate tables; Met Office). The rain is orographic: summer monsoon air from
the Bay of Bengal is forced up the Khasi Hills and falls mostly June–September (NASA Earth Observatory; Britannica
"Cherrapunji", "Meghalaya"). Nearby Cherrapunji recorded 26,470 mm in the twelve months to July 1861, the most ever in a
year (Guinness); Khasi villagers carry woven rain shields called knups (Wikipedia "Mawsynram").
