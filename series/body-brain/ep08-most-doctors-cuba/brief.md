# Brief — Body & Brain #8 · Which nation has the MOST doctors per person?

## Hook fact
Cuba has about 9.5 physicians per 1,000 people (World Bank / WHO, 2021), the highest ratio in the world; Germany has 4.5 and the
United States 3.7 (2022).

## Show it
Night-lights opener over the Atlantic with the question; three flag options. Reveal: Cuba fills red with a pulse, the Havana pin on
"doctors", the tag "Cuba · 9.5 per 1,000" and the 9.5 PER 1,000 card. Pull out over the Atlantic: three bars grow
— 9.5 / 4.5 / 3.7 — with a stethoscope, the US flag-ball thinks on "States". Push back onto the island: a
counter that starts at 106,131 and FALLS to 75,364 (doctors on the island, 2021 → 2024), the island sticker, then a door on "left".
Pull out over the Caribbean: the 37,000 ABROAD card, the plane sticker, the "doctors in 50+ countries" tag, Cuba pulses once more.

## Different from
#7 (diabetes) was compare blocks + a rising counter + a birthday pin; this is bars + a counter that goes DOWN — the first falling
counter in the series — with no second country tag and no date card. Show-set {bars, counter} is unlike the last six
(ep23 route/counter/split, ep07 compare/counter, ep22 bars/route/split, ep14 compare/rise/flash, ep17 compare/route, ep21 bars/route/travel).

- 20 s — a six-digit number ticking DOWN on screen next to a door — the beat the comments will argue about.
