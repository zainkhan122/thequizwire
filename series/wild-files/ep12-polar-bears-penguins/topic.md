## Why this, why now
It is the single most common animal-geography misconception (every Christmas ad puts them together), and it is a MAP fact:
polar bears live only around the Arctic, penguins only in the Southern Hemisphere (the Galápagos penguin touches the equator).
The explanation is a globe story — two hemispheres, one spin — which no backlog episode has.

## Viewer takeaway
"Polar bears and penguins live at opposite ends of the planet — they have never met outside a cartoon."

## Question shape
true-or-false — first T/F on the channel; the options are TRUE / FALSE (+ "only in zoos" as the tempting third), so the
misconception does the work; the map reveal is the two poles.

## Options and why they fool
