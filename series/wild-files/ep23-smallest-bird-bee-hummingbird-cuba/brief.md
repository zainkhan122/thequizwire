# Brief — Wild Files #23 · Which country is home to the SMALLEST bird?

## Hook fact
The bee hummingbird (Mellisuga helenae) is the smallest living bird and lives only in Cuba and the Isla de la Juventud:
about 5.5–6.1 cm long and about 1.6–2 g, lighter than a US penny (Britannica "Bee hummingbird"; Guinness World Records
"Smallest bird"; Cornell Lab, Birds of the World). Its wings beat about 80 times a second in normal flight and it may
visit up to 1,500 flowers a day (Cornell; Wikipedia). The egg is about the size of a coffee bean and the cup nest about
2.5 cm across (Britannica). For scale a house sparrow is about 14–16 cm and a rock pigeon about 29–37 cm (Britannica).
The Zapata Swamp in the south-west is one of its best-known strongholds (Audubon).
