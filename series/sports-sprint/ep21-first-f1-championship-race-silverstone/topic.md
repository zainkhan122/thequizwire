## Why this, why now
The hook is the venue: the World Championship opened on 13 May 1950 on a circuit laid over the runways of a wartime
Wellington bomber base, with King George VI in the grandstand; Giuseppe Farina won in an Alfa Romeo 158 and clinched the
first title at Monza in September (Formula1.com; Silverstone Circuit; Britannica). The 7-round season against this
year's 24 gives a THEN / NOW split, and the Silverstone-to-Monza line gives the map a journey. #20 was a trophy count;
this is an origin with a route and a split.
