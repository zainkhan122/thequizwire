# Which river carries the MOST water?

## Why this, why now
Length and flow are different records: the Nile is the longest but carries about 3,000 m³/s; the Amazon pours about
209,000 m³/s into the Atlantic — roughly a fifth of all river water reaching the oceans (USGS; Britannica); the Congo is a
distant second at 41,000. The mouth is over 320 km wide, and in 1500 Pinzón found fresh water out of sight of land and
named it the Sweet Sea (Britannica). EASY by answer, hard by scale — the bars do the work.
