## Why this, why now
The biggest animal does not have the biggest eyes. The colossal squid's eye is about 27 cm across (Te Papa; Nilsson et al.
2012), an ostrich's 5 cm is bigger than its brain, and the blue whale manages only 15 cm. The record specimen came up on a
toothfish line in the Ross Sea in 2007 and the reason the eyes are so big — seeing a sperm whale coming a kilometre down —
gives the Short a dark, cinematic close.
