## Why this, why now
Every November the Davis Cup trends for one week and the explainers all cover the format. Nobody tells the origin: four
Harvard students dared Britain to a match, one of them bought the trophy himself, and the British sailed over to lose 3–0.
That is a story with a map in it (the Atlantic crossing) and a punchline in numbers (USA 32 titles).

## Viewer takeaway
"The Davis Cup started in Boston in 1900 as a student challenge — and the guy it's named after paid for the trophy."
