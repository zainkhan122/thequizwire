# Brief — Body & Brain #6 · Which country records the MOST stroke deaths every year?

## Hook fact
Not the United States and not the most populous country: China records about 2.6 million stroke deaths a year — one in three of the 7.3 million worldwide, sixteen times the US figure of 165,393. World Stroke Day is 29 October; FAST (face, arm, speech, time) is the takeaway.

## Show it
A Pacific-wide Mercator stage with the three giants; the camera drifts over Asia under the question. Reveal = China fills red with a pulse, "China" tag, flag-ball reaction. Then the camera flies across the Pacific so the US and China share the frame: the US lights dim, a counter climbs to 165,000 and a US flag sticker lands; on "16" China pulses hard and the wow character pops. Pull out to the whole stage: the world counter runs to 7,300,000 and the globe sticker drops. Back onto China for the World Stroke Day card, the brain sticker on "FAST", the FAST card and the phone sticker on "time".

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — fill-pulse China, tag, answer card.
2. s05 compare — US second-highlight + "United States · 165,000" tag; counter 0 → 165,000; US flag sticker; China pulse on "16".
3. s06 world — counter 0 → 7,300,000 "stroke deaths a year, worldwide"; globe sticker on "Earth".
4. s07 takeaway — WORLD STROKE DAY card on "Day", 🧠 on "FAST", FAST card on "face", 📞 on "time".

## Different from
Body & Brain #5 (heart transplant) was a colour-lift history piece with a stopped counter, a rising photo and a heartbeat sticker. This one is present-tense statistics: two running counters at different scales, a trans-Pacific fly, no photo, and the only Body & Brain so far whose last beat is an instruction (FAST) rather than a fact.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — satellite stage drifting over Asia under the question; MOST in yellow.
- 6 s — the three flags are up and the timer runs; most viewers have said "America" or "India".
- 16 s — the camera lands on the US with its counter stopping at 165,000 while the voice says "sixteen times that" and China throbs — the wrong guess gets its number.
