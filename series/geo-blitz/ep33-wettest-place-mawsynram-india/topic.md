## Why this, why now
Everyone guesses a rainforest. The record for average rainfall is Mawsynram, a hill village in Meghalaya, India, at
about 11,871 mm a year (Guinness; IMD), roughly five times the Amazon's Manaus. The reason is a wall: monsoon air off
the Bay of Bengal is forced up the Khasi Hills and drops everything in a few summer months; the town next door once
took 26,470 mm in twelve months. A "where is" stem keeps the opening away from the which-country run.
