## Why this, why now
Team USA set its own Winter record (12 golds, 33 medals) and hosts Italy had their best Games (10 golds, 30), yet Norway won
the table outright: 18 golds — the most by any nation at a single Winter Games — and 41 medals (Olympics.com; USA Today).
One Norwegian, Johannes Klæbo, took 6 golds, more than Canada's 5. The punchline is population: under 6 million against 340
million. Sports Sprint's last two were football; this is snow.
