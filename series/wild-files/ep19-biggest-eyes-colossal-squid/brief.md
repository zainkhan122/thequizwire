# Brief — Wild Files #19 · Which creature has the most ENORMOUS eyes?

## Hook fact
The colossal squid's eye measures about 27 cm in diameter, the largest of any animal (Te Papa; Nilsson et al. 2012, Current
Biology). Ostrich about 5 cm, bigger than its brain (Smithsonian); blue whale about 15 cm. The record 495 kg specimen was
caught on a toothfish longline by the San Aspiring in the Ross Sea in February 2007 and is displayed at Te Papa, Wellington.
Nilsson et al. argue the eyes evolved to detect the bioluminescent glow stirred up by sperm whales hunting at 500–1,000 m+.
