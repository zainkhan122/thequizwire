# Brief — Geo Blitz #20 · Where is the COLDEST capital city on Earth?

## Hook fact
Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia: the only national capital whose yearly average temperature is below freezing. January nights pass −30 °C; Astana is the runner-up; the city sits about 1,350 m up on a plateau, and about half of all Mongolians (≈ 1.6 million) live in it.

## Show it
A Central-Asia satellite stage at night-lights under the question, the three flags over their countries. Reveal = Mongolia fills ice-blue with a pulse, "Mongolia" tag, the Ulaanbaatar pin drops on the city's name, BELOW 0 °C card. Then the January counter sinks to −30 with a freezing-face sticker; the camera pulls west so Kazakhstan shares the frame, it lights dim with the Astana pin and "Astana · 2nd" tag; a shrug on "Ottawa". Push in on the city: the altitude counter climbs to 1,350 with a mountain sticker and the "1,350 m up" tag. Settle on the country: a THE CITY / THE REST split slides in (1.6 million in Ulaanbaatar vs 1.9 million everywhere else) — the fact is shown as a split, not told on a card; people sticker, the cool flag-ball.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — fill-pulse Mongolia, tag, Ulaanbaatar pin, BELOW 0 °C card.
2. s05 cold + runner-up — counter 0 → −30 °C; Kazakhstan second-highlight, Astana pin + tag; 🤷 on "Ottawa".
3. s06 why — counter 0 → 1,350 "metres above sea level"; 🏔 sticker; "1,350 m up" tag.
4. s07 scale — HALF THE NATION card (1.6 million); 👥 sticker; pulse + cool flag-ball. — shown as the THE CITY / THE REST split panel

## Different from
Geo Blitz #19 (Oymyakon) was a village pin with a record card, a bars chart against a freezer, and a school-threshold ending. This one is a capital question with a runner-up beat, an altitude why-beat and a population kicker — no record, no bars, and a west-then-in camera path rather than the east push.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — night-lights Asia drifting under the question; COLDEST in yellow.
- 6 s — three flags up, timer running; most viewers have said "Canada".
- 15 s — the counter sinks to −30 while the voice says "January nights" — the number that makes the answer land.
