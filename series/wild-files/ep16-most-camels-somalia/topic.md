# Which nation is home to the MOST camels?

## Signal
suggest: "which country has the most camels" (YouTube autocomplete, checked 2026-11-05 in the "which country has the most
[animal]" family alongside tigers/elephants/lions in review/signals.md); wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camel;
our stats (dispatch/stats.md): Wild Files "MOST [animal]" country questions (#8 tigers, #11 elephants, #13 lions) are the
series' most-watched; Somalia is a country we have never lit.

## Why this, why now
Everyone says Saudi Arabia. FAO stock data put Somalia first with more than 7 million camels — the largest herd on Earth,
in a country of about 18 million people — ahead of Sudan (~5 M) and Kenya (~3 M); Saudi Arabia keeps under 2 million
(FAO; Worldmapper). The twist: the largest WILD herd (≈ 300,000 feral dromedaries) lives in Australia, where camels are not
native (DCCEEW). A second-colour country (Arabia) wiped off the screen is the device.

## Viewer takeaway
"Somalia has the most camels (7 M+); Saudi Arabia under 2 M; Australia has the biggest feral herd (~300,000)."

## Question shape
which-country, record superlative ("Which nation is home to the MOST camels?"); flag options; reads verbatim as the hook;
hook = misconception (Arabia is the trap); mystery-zoom-out opener from the Arabian Sea so no candidate is in frame.

## Options and why they fool
A Saudi Arabia — camels mean Arabia in most heads · B Australia — the "feral camels" fact is half-remembered and sounds like
a trick answer · C Somalia (answer) — almost nobody ranks Somalia first in anything.

- "Which animal has the most domesticated numbers" — a chicken answer; not a map question.
- "Where do wild camels live" — Australia/Mongolia; better as the closing beat here.
