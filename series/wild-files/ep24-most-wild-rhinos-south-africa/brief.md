# Brief — Wild Files #24 · Where do MOST wild rhinos live?

## Hook fact
The world holds about 27,000 wild rhinos: roughly 17,500 white, 6,400 black, 4,000 greater one-horned, about 76
Javan and fewer than 50 Sumatran (International Rhino Foundation, State of the Rhino 2024). South Africa holds
around 16,000 of them — about 13,000 white and 2,000–2,500 black (DFFE 2024) — roughly 6 in 10 of all rhinos. Kenya
has about 2,000 (KWS 2023); India about 4,000, almost all greater one-horned, around 2,600 of them in Kaziranga
(Assam Forest Department 2022). Kruger National Park holds the largest single rhino population in the world
(SANParks).
