# Brief — Sports Sprint #20 · Which nation has the MOST Rugby World Cups?

## Hook fact
South Africa has won four Rugby World Cups (1995, 2007, 2019, 2023); New Zealand three, Australia two, England one (World
Rugby; Britannica). The 1995 final was at Ellis Park, Johannesburg, a year after apartheid ended, Mandela presenting the cup
in a Springbok jersey; the 2023 final in Paris finished 12–11 over New Zealand (BBC Sport).
