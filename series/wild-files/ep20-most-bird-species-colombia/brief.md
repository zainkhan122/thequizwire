# Brief — Wild Files #20 · Which country has the MOST bird species?

## Hook fact
Colombia's national checklist stands at about 1,969 bird species, the most of any country and roughly a fifth of the
world's (Colombian Committee of Ornithological Records; BirdLife International). Peru is second at about 1,890, Brazil
third at about 1,860, Indonesia fourth (Wikipedia, List of birds by country). The diversity comes from the three Andean
cordilleras and the deep valleys between them plus Caribbean and Pacific coasts (Britannica). Colombia has more
hummingbird species than any other country, over 160 (Wikipedia; Britannica "Hummingbird"); the Sierra Nevada de Santa
Marta is one of the richest endemic bird areas on Earth (BirdLife EBA factsheet).
