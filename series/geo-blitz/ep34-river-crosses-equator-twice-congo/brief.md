# Brief — Geo Blitz #34 · Which river crosses the Equator TWICE?

## Hook fact
The Congo is the only major river to cross the Equator twice: the upper river (Lualaba) flows north over the line near
Kisangani, the great bend swings it west, and it recrosses the Equator southbound near Mbandaka on its way to the
Atlantic (Britannica "Congo River"; NASA Earth Observatory "The Congo River"). It is about 4,700 km long with the
second-largest discharge on Earth, about 41,000 m³/s, and USGS surveys of the lower river in 2008 measured depths over
220 m, the deepest known in any river (Oberg et al., USGS Open-File Report 2009-1164). Kinshasa and Brazzaville face
each other across Pool Malebo, the closest pair of national capitals in the world (Britannica "Kinshasa"; Wikipedia
"Brazzaville"). The Amazon runs a few degrees south of the Equator and never crosses it; the Nile rises near it and
flows north away (Britannica).

## Show it
Split-candidates opener: the camera visits the Amazon mouth, the Nile valley and the Congo basin as each option is
named, then settles wide. Reveal = route-draw: a dashed yellow Equator draws across the basin, then the river draws
itself north over the line and back south, with the TWICE card. Beat two holds the basin: the Brazilian flag-ball
thinks on "Amazon", an Egyptian one on "Nile", and the two crossing tags land on "arc" as the river re-draws. Beat
three pushes toward the lower river: a boat rides the drawn line, the depth counter runs to 220 m on "deepest" with
an anchor sticker. Beat four tightens on the pool: two pins land either side of the water on "capitals", the
2 CAPITALS card on "across", the Congolese flag-ball grins on "closest", pins leave.

## Different from
#33 (Mawsynram) is bars + flash; #32 (Nepal) bars + split; #28 (Amazon) route + bars + split + travel. This is the
first Geo Blitz whose SHOW set is {route, travel, counter} with no chart bars at all: a line of latitude drawn as a
route, a river drawn over it, a boat along it, and one counter for the depth — the geometry is the argument.
