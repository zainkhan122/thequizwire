## Why this, why now
Most viewers assume Europe, or Brazil because of the titles. The first World Cup was played in Uruguay in July 1930,
entirely in Montevideo, by thirteen teams (FIFA; Britannica). The hosts were Olympic champions, built a stadium that
was finished days after the opening match, and most of Europe refused the two-week voyage; the few who came sailed
on one ship that picked up Brazil's squad in Rio. The hosts won the first final. A sea voyage is a map story.

## Viewer takeaway
"The first World Cup was in Uruguay in 1930, all in Montevideo, 13 teams; Italy hosted the second; the hosts were
Olympic champions, built the Estadio Centenario for it, and beat Argentina 4–2 in the final; most of Europe stayed home."
