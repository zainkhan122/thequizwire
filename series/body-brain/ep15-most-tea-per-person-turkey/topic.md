## Why this, why now
Ask anyone and they say Britain. Per person it is Turkey, at roughly 3.2 kg a year (Euromonitor via Statista; FAO),
with Ireland second and the UK third; China, which grew the leaf first, drinks well under a kilo per head. The twist
is that Turkey grows almost all of it itself, on the wet Black Sea hills of Rize, and pours it dozens of times a day in
tulip glasses. A "who" stem keeps the opening away from the which-country run.
