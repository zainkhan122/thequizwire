# Brief — Wild Files #21 · Which animal has the STRONGEST bite?

## Hook fact
The saltwater crocodile has the strongest bite ever directly measured on a living animal: 16,460 N, about 3,700 psi,
recorded from a 5.2 m animal by Erickson et al. (PLoS ONE, 2012; Guinness World Records). A lion bites at around
1,000 psi and a hippo is estimated at about 1,800 psi (National Geographic). Crocodilian jaw muscles are huge for
closing and weak for opening, so the mouth can be held shut with a band (Britannica; National Geographic). The largest
live in the rivers and estuaries of northern Australia, exceed 6 m and ambush prey at the water's edge (Australian
Museum; Parks Australia Kakadu).
