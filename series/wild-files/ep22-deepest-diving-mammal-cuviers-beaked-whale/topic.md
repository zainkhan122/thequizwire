# Which mammal dives the DEEPEST?

## Why this, why now
Everyone says sperm whale (the squid hunter) or elephant seal. The record belongs to Cuvier's beaked whale: 2,992 m off
California (Schorr et al. 2014) and, on a separate dive off Cape Hatteras, 222 minutes under water (Quick et al. 2020). The
mechanism is the twist: the lungs collapse on purpose. Two US coasts give the map two stops instead of one highlight.

## Viewer takeaway
"The deepest-diving mammal is Cuvier's beaked whale: almost 3,000 m down, and once 3 hours 42 minutes on one breath; a
sperm whale reaches about 2,250 m, an elephant seal about 2,400 m; it survives because its lungs collapse and its muscles
store the oxygen."
