# Which is the FLATTEST country on Earth?

## Signal
suggest: "flattest country in the world" (YouTube autocomplete, checked 2026-11-11 in the "which is the … country"
family in review/signals.md); wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_highest_point; our stats

## Why this, why now
The misconception trap: everyone says the Netherlands because a quarter of it is below sea level, yet its mainland rises
to 322 m; Denmark reaches 171 m; the Maldives' highest natural point is about 5 m across 1,190 coral islands (CIA World
Factbook; Britannica). Average ground is a metre and a half, so Malé hides behind a 3 m sea wall, and in 2009 the cabinet
met underwater to make the point (The Guardian). A country that is barely there, told on a map that has to zoom to ×8 to
find the capital.

## Viewer takeaway

is low but reaches 322 m."
