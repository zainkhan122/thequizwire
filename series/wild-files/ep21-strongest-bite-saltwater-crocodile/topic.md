# Which animal has the STRONGEST bite?

## Why this, why now
Most viewers say hippo (the gape) or shark. The strongest bite ever directly measured on a living animal belongs to the
saltwater crocodile: 16,460 N, about 3,700 psi, recorded by Erickson's team in 2012 (PLoS ONE; Guinness). A lion is
under a third of that, a hippo about half. The twist is mechanical: the muscles only close, so a rubber band can hold
the mouth shut. The map takes us to the estuaries of northern Australia where the biggest live.

## Viewer takeaway
"The saltwater crocodile has the strongest measured bite (about 3,700 psi); a hippo is about half, a lion under a third;
the jaw muscles close hard but open weakly; the biggest live in northern Australia's rivers and estuaries."
