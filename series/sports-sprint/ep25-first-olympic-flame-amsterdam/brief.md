# Brief — Sports Sprint #25 · In which city did the Olympic flame FIRST burn?

## Hook fact
The first Olympic flame of the modern Games burned at Amsterdam 1928, in a bowl on the Olympic Stadium's Marathon Tower
(architect Jan Wils), lit by an employee of the city's electricity utility to show where the Games were (Wikipedia: Olympic
flame; 1928 Summer Olympics; Britannica). Athens 1896 had no flame. The torch relay was invented by Carl Diem for Berlin 1936:
lit by the sun in Olympia on 20 July, carried 3,187 km by 3,331 runners in twelve days through seven countries, filmed by
Leni Riefenstahl for the Nazi regime (Wikipedia: 1936 Summer Olympics torch relay; Olympia (1938 film); IOC).
