# Brief — Sports Sprint #15 · Which city hosted the FIRST Davis Cup in 1900?

## Hook fact
The first Davis Cup tie was played 8–10 August 1900 at the Longwood Cricket Club in Boston after four Harvard players
challenged Britain; the United States won 3–0 on grass, and Dwight Davis bought the silver trophy himself.

## Show it
A North-Atlantic stage (bbox −100…20 / 25…62) drifting under the question so both shores are in frame from the start. Reveal
= the United States fills and pulses, "Boston" tag, Longwood pin on "Longwood", 1900 card. Then the crossing: pull out to the
whole ocean, the British Isles light dim, Liverpool pin, a steamship route draws west while a ship sticker rides it to Boston
(geo.travel); on "lost" the US pulses and the sad GB flag-ball appears. Then back to Boston: the Library of Congress portrait
of Dwight Davis rises out of the United States, the trophy sticker pops. Finish wide with the all-time title table (USA 32,
Australia 28, GB 10, France 10) growing over the ocean and the wow US flag-ball.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — fill-pulse USA, Boston tag, Longwood pin, "1900 · Longwood Cricket Club · USA 3 – 0" card.
2. s05 crossing — British Isles dim highlight, Liverpool pin, route draw + ship travel Liverpool → Boston, US pulse + sad GB ball on "lost", tennis ball on "grass".
3. s06 Davis — photo rise (PD, LoC Harris & Ewing) of Dwight F. Davis, trophy sticker on "trophy", Longwood pin back on "name".
4. s07 titles — bars USA / Australia / GB / France, trophy on "Australia", wow US ball on "nobody".

## Different from
Ep14 (cricket) was bars → counter → fly to India; ep13 (marathon) route-draw reveal + travel on the course + split panels;
ep12 (Brazil) bar race + counter. This is the only Sports explanation whose spine is an ocean crossing (route + travel across
water, not a race course), and the first photo rise in the series.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — the question lands on an Atlantic stage already drifting; the US is NOT highlighted before the reveal.
- 7 s — the camera is sliding toward the American coast as the third option is read, then 3 s of silence.
- 15 s — the ship is mid-Atlantic on the drawn route; the British Isles glow behind it.
