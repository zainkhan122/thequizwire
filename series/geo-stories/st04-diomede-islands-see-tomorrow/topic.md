# The island where you can see tomorrow (Diomede Islands)

## Signal
suggest: "diomede islands" (YouTube autocomplete under "island where you can see tomorrow", checked 2026-11-18); wikipedia:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diomede_Islands; the "Tomorrow Island / Yesterday Isle" line circulates as a text fact with no
map, so a satellite close-up with the Date Line actually drawn between the two rocks is the version nobody has seen.

## Why this, why now
Two islands less than four kilometres apart, with the International Date Line between them, so one is almost a full day
ahead of the other; one is a US village, the other a Russian military post. The map does the whole job: plunge onto the
strait, two neon islands, the red line between them, flags, the gap measured, the people on each side.

## Viewer takeaway
"Big Diomede (Russia) and Little Diomede (USA) are about 3.8 km apart; the Date Line runs between them, so Big Diomede is
up to 21 hours ahead. Big Diomede's villagers were moved to the mainland in 1948; about 80 people live on Little Diomede."
