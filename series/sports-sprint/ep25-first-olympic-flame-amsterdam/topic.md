## Why this, why now
Everyone assumes the flame is as old as the Games and starts in Greece. It does not: the first Olympic flame burned in
Amsterdam in 1928, in a bowl on a stadium tower, lit by an electricity worker; the 1896 Athens Games had none, and the relay
from Olympia was invented for Berlin 1936 as a propaganda show (3,187 km, 3,331 runners, twelve days, seven countries). One
small host lifts into its flag, then the map runs the whole 1936 route.

## Viewer takeaway
"The first Olympic flame was in Amsterdam 1928, on the stadium's Marathon Tower; Athens 1896 had no flame; the torch relay
from Olympia began only in 1936 for Berlin — 3,187 km, 3,331 runners, twelve days — and every relay since copies it."
