# Nearest humans: in space (Point Nemo)

## Signal
suggest: "point nemo" (YouTube autocomplete under "most remote place on earth", checked 2026-11-18); wikipedia:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pole_of_inaccessibility; news: every controlled re-entry (ISS deorbit plan, Tiangong,
large rocket stages) puts "spacecraft cemetery" back in the headlines. Our catalogue has no ocean-only episode and no
story where the map device is a to-scale distance circle.

## Why this, why now
"The nearest people are in orbit" is a sentence everybody wants to disprove, and the map proves it in one frame: the
2,688 km circle around Point Nemo touches exactly three uninhabited specks of land, the space station passes at about
400 km, and that emptiness is why agencies aim dead spacecraft here. It widens Geo Stories from borders to ocean and
space with the same engine (pins, routes, counters, scalebar).

## Viewer takeaway
"Point Nemo (48°52.6′S 123°23.6′W) is about 2,688 km from the nearest land (Ducie Island, Motu Nui, Maher Island); the
ISS at ~400 km is at times nearer than anyone on land; 260+ spacecraft including Mir were deorbited into the area."

## Question shape
number-nobody-believes — one distance nobody expects; mystery-zoom-out opener (we start on open water and pull back
until the circle and the three islands appear), counters for the distance and the spacecraft, scalebar for honesty.

## Twist
The loneliness is the point: because nothing and nobody is there, it is the safest place on Earth to drop a space
station. The close returns to the opening noun ("neighbours only in orbit").
