# Which country is home to the SMALLEST bird?

## Why this, why now
The reflex answer is a rainforest country. The smallest bird on Earth, the bee hummingbird, lives only in Cuba: about
6 cm long and about 2 g, lighter than a coin (Britannica; Guinness; Cornell Lab). It beats its wings about 80 times a
second, can visit 1,500 flowers a day, and lays an egg the size of a coffee bean in a nest the width of a thimble. A
"which country is home to" stem with an island answer gives the series a map that is one small island, not a
continent.
