## Why this, why now
China has won 37 of the 42 Olympic golds ever awarded in table tennis, so almost everyone picks China. The game began as
an after-dinner parlour pastime in Victorian England in the 1880s — books for a net, a cigar-box lid for a bat — and the
first commercial sets and rules were sold in London around 1901 (Britannica; ITTF). The "inventors have never won a
gold" bar at the end is the kicker.
