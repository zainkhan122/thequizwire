## Why this, why now
Most people answer tortoise. Nielsen et al. (Science, 2016) dated 28 Greenland sharks by radiocarbon in the eye lens: the
largest female 392 ± 120 years (272–512), maturity at least 156 ± 22, growth about 1 cm a year. Bowhead whales pass 200 (NOAA);
Jonathan the tortoise is about 190 (Guinness). Even the cautious end of the estimate puts the shark's birth before 1776 — the
line that makes the number land. Wild Files' last three were speed, size and habitat; this is time.
