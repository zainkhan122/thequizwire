# Where is the LONGEST land border on Earth?

## Hook fact
Canada–USA: 8,891 km, one unbroken line from ocean to ocean to ocean — and the runner-up is more than 1,200 km behind.

## Show it
The line draws itself across the continent on the reveal (already the strongest thing in this Short — keep it).
Then a three-bar race against the two wrong answers so the gap is visible, not spoken. Then the camera leaves the lower 48
and flies to Alaska while a kilometre counter runs 0 → 2,475 — the part people forget. Last, a dive into the Vermont–Quebec
forest: one pin at Derby Line, one card for the 6 m strip that is literally cut through the trees to keep the line visible.

## Beat order
1. Bars vs Kazakhstan–Russia and Argentina–Chile (settles the "by how much?") → 2. Alaska flight + counter (the surprise
second border) → 3. Forest strip card + pin (a human-scale image to end on). Big → far → close.

## Different from
Geo #17 (Baikal) opens on a depth counter and ends on a river route chase; here the route is the reveal itself, and the
explanation is bars → counter → pin. No comparison blocks, no sticker.

## Swipe test
8 s: the answer flag lifts and the border line starts drawing coast to coast — motion carries the reveal.
15 s: three bars racing; the winner's bar is still growing when the others stop.
23 s: the camera is sliding north to Alaska with the counter sprinting — a second number nobody expected.

## Devices
route (reveal), bars, counter, pin, card, cam
