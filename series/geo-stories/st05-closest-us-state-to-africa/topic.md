# The US state closest to Africa is not Florida (Maine · great circle)

## Signal
suggest: "closest us state to africa" (YouTube/Google autocomplete, checked 2026-11-19); wikipedia:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_points_of_the_United_States; the "it's Maine, not Florida" line circulates as a
trivia caption with no map — a satellite view with both great circles actually drawn is the version nobody has seen.

## Why this, why now
A guess nearly everyone gets wrong, settled by two lines on a globe: Quoddy Head in Maine to the Moroccan coast is
shorter than anything from Florida because the shortest path on a sphere bends toward the pole and Africa's bulge leans
north. The map does the whole job: Florida pinned and stamped NO, a plane riding the Maine line, two counters, the pull-out.

## Viewer takeaway
"Maine is the US state closest to Africa — about 5,076 km from West Quoddy Head to El Beddouza, Morocco; Florida to the
nearest African shore is over 6,300 km. Great circles bend north."

## Question shape
guess-then-reveal — plunge opener onto Florida, the NO stamp, the Maine ride, counters, the 'bends north' climax card.

## Twist
The close: not Florida, Maine — and the reason is that the Earth is a ball, not a flat map.
