# Brief — Geo Blitz #21 · Where are MOST of the world's lakes?

## Hook fact
Canada has about 880,000 lakes above 0.1 sq km — more than the rest of the world combined (≈ 62 % of 1.42 million). Second
place Russia has about 200,000. Finland, "land of a thousand lakes", is nowhere near at that size.

## Show it
Mystery opener: the camera starts tight on the lake-pocked Shield (a blue-speckled satellite texture, no country readable) and
pulls out under the question until Canada's shape appears. Reveal = Canada fills blue with a pulse, "Canada" tag, MOST ON EARTH
card. Then a wide pull so Canada and Russia share one frame while a three-row bar chart grows — 879,800 / 201,200 / 102,500
— the bars are the argument, the voice only reads two numbers; Russia lights dim with "Russia · 2nd"; a shrug on "Finland". Push
back onto Canada: snowflake lands on "ice", the ice-age tag on "scraped", a wave on "hollow". Finally deep into the Northwest
Territories: Great Bear and Great Slave pins, a line draws between them and a canoe sticker paddles it (geo.travel) while the
voice says most Canadians have never seen them; the wow flag-ball pops.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — fill-pulse Canada, tag, MOST ON EARTH card.
2. s05 scale — chart.bars_in Canada / Russia / United States; Russia second-highlight + tag; 🤷 on "Finland".
3. s06 why — ❄ on "ice", "ice age · 10,000 yrs ago" tag on "scraped", 🌊 + pulse on "hollow".
4. s07 kicker — two lake pins, route_draw Slave → Bear, 🛶 travels the line, wow flag-ball on "seen".

## Different from
#20 (coldest capital) was a split-candidates opener with counters and a city-vs-rest split; #19 had bars against a freezer; #17
(Baikal) drew a river. This one opens on a mystery zoom-out, uses bars for a count (not a temperature), and is the first Geo
Blitz with a travel beat — a sticker riding a drawn line — so the show-device set {bars, route, travel} is new in the last 12.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — blue-speckled unknown terrain pulling out under MOST in yellow; what is this place?
- 7 s — flags up, timer running; most viewers have said Finland.
- 16 s — the 879,800 bar is still growing while the Russia bar stops at a fraction of it — the picture that makes the answer land.
- 27 s — a canoe moves across the map between two pins; motion, not a card.

## Devices
mystery-zoom-out opener · fill-pulse highlight · fact card ×1 · chart.bars_in (3 rows) · second highlight layer (Russia) · tag labels
×3 · geo.pin_in ×2 (Great Bear, Great Slave) · geo.route_draw + geo.travel (canoe along the schematic line) · stickers 💧 🤷 ❄ 🌊 🛶 +

## Media & Pace
No photograph: the lakes read on the satellite basemap itself and the canoe ride is the visual; Commons lake photos add nothing
the map does not already show. Target 85–90 words, spoken numerals 880,000 / 200,000 only, ≥ 17 motion events / 10 s, ≤ 38 s.
