# Brief — Body & Brain #13 · Which country saw the FIRST human heart transplant?

## Hook fact
Christiaan Barnard performed the first human-to-human heart transplant at Groote Schuur Hospital, Cape Town, on 3 December
1967, with a team of about 30 (Heart of Cape Town Museum; Brink & Hassoulas 2009, CVJA). The patient, grocer Louis
Washkansky, lived 18 days; the second, Philip Blaiberg, 593 days (CVJA). Norman Shumway's Stanford team had developed the
technique and did the first US transplant on 6 January 1968 (Stanford Medicine). Median survival today is about 12.5 years
(ISHLT registry, Khush et al. 2019). The donor, Denise Darvall, 25, was struck by a car; her father consented that night.
