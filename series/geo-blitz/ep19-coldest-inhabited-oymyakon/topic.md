# The COLDEST inhabited place on Earth is in which country?

## Signal
trends: Google Trends US 2026-10-26 https://trends.google.com/trending/rss?geo=US — "polar vortex forecast" (500+ searches, review/signals.md) — the first cold-snap week of the
season, when "coldest place on earth" climbs every year; YouTube autocomplete seed "coldest" → "coldest place on earth",
"coldest city in the world".

## Why this, why now
Half of North America is reading a polar-vortex forecast this week. The sharpest way to put that in perspective is a real
village where −50 °C is a school day — and most viewers will guess Canada or Greenland, because "nearer the pole = colder".
The surprise is that the record is deep inside a continent, not on the ice cap.

## Viewer takeaway
"The coldest village on Earth is in Siberia, hit −67.7 °C, and the kids still go to school at −50."

## Question shape
which-country with a superlative the viewer *thinks* they can reason out from latitude — a where-is answer (a village pin)
inside a which-country frame. Not a stem used in the last five (those were "which of / most recently", "the first", "how many",
"polar bears never", "which country tallest").

## Options and why they fool
A Canada — Snag, Yukon holds North America's record (−63 °C) and "Canada = cold" is the reflex · B Russia (answer: Oymyakon,
Sakha Republic) · C Greenland — the ice cap is where people picture the cold, but the inhabited coast is far milder.

## Rejected alternatives

  is a continent, not a country; the quiz collapses at second 2.
