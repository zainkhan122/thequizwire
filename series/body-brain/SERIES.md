# Body & Brain (`body-brain`)

Anatomy and neuroscience questions answered on public-domain plates with animated call-outs and one counted number.

Numbering: feed #3 → next #4 (`series/series.json` is the counter; `tools/new_episode.py body-brain …` bumps it).

## Format rules
- Stage: PD plate (Gray's 1918 / OpenStax CC BY) with `callouts` + `counter`; no stock b-roll
- Numbers come from NIH/NLM/WHO/OpenStax (research.json `record` tier)
- Medical guardrails: no diagnosis/advice wording; 'on average', 'typically' where the literature says so
- Engine status: `callouts`/`counter` are Phase-2 work in engine/charts.py (bars exist)

## Sources
See `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` (Body & Brain section). Every fact → `research.json` claim; every file → `media.json`.
