# Where is the DEEPEST lake on Earth?

## Hook fact
One Siberian lake holds about a fifth of all the unfrozen fresh water on the planet — more than all five Great Lakes together.

## Show it
Not said, counted and compared. First a big counter rolls 0 → 1,642 m while the camera sinks onto the lake (the depth
becomes a feeling, not a number). Then a proportional comparison: a block for Baikal next to a block for all five Great
Lakes combined, Baikal's taller — the "wait, what?" moment. Finally 336-in / 1-out: pins pop along the shore, then one
route traces the Angara out of the lake while the camera chases it.

## Beat order
1. Depth counter sinking to 1,642 m (sets scale) → 2. Baikal vs Great Lakes blocks (the surprise, biggest first) →
3. Rivers in / one out with the Angara traced (motion payoff, ends on a moving camera into the CTA).
Escalation: a number → a comparison that reframes the number → a map animation that makes it personal.

## Different from
Geo #16 was a label-and-pin walk along a line; this one has no fact-card walk at all — a counter, a comparison chart and a
route chase, with only one card.

## Swipe test
8 s: the reveal — Russia lifts, flag-balls react, banner lands; they stay for "how deep".
15 s: the counter is still rolling up and the camera is sinking onto the lake; nobody leaves mid-count.
22 s: two blocks grow, Baikal overtakes the Great Lakes — the surprise; the Angara chase starts right after.

## Devices
counter, compare, pin, route, card (one), character, cam
