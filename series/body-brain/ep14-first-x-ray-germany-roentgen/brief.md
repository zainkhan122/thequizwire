# Brief — Body & Brain #14 · Which country made the FIRST X-ray image?

## Hook fact
Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen, professor of physics at the University of Würzburg (Bavaria), discovered X-rays on 8 November
1895 (Nobel Prize Outreach; University of Würzburg). On 22 December 1895 he radiographed his wife Anna Bertha's hand —
bones and wedding ring, an exposure of about 15 minutes; she reportedly said "I have seen my death" (Deutsches
Röntgen-Museum; Britannica). Doctors were locating bullets and fractures within weeks (Britannica "X-ray"); Röntgen
refused to patent the discovery and received the first Nobel Prize in Physics in 1901 (Nobel Prize Outreach).
