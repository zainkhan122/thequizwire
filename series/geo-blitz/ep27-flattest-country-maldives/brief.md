# Brief — Geo Blitz #27 · Which is the FLATTEST country on Earth?

## Hook fact
The Maldives is the lowest, flattest country: highest natural point about 5 m (CIA World Factbook: the 8th tee on
Villingili), 1,190 coral islands in 26 atolls, average ground about 1.5 m (Britannica). The Netherlands' mainland high
point is the Vaalserberg, 322 m; Denmark's Møllehøj, 171 m (CIA). Malé is ringed by a 3 m sea wall built with Japanese
aid after the 1987 floods (JICA). On 17 October 2009 President Nasheed's cabinet met underwater in scuba gear to sign a
declaration on sea-level rise before Copenhagen (The Guardian).
