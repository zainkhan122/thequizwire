## Why this, why now
Everyone has had an X-ray; almost nobody knows the first one was a wife's hand with her wedding ring, exposed for a
quarter of an hour in a Bavarian physics institute, or that she said "I have seen my death". The discoverer refused to
patent the rays and took the very first Nobel Prize in Physics (Nobel Prize Outreach). The actual plate is public
domain, so the Short can show the real picture rising out of the map, not a reconstruction.

## Viewer takeaway
"X-rays were discovered in Germany (Würzburg, 1895) by a physics professor; the first image was his wife's hand with
her ring; he refused to patent it and won the first Nobel Prize in Physics."

## Options and why they fool

B Germany (answer) — the discovery is famous, the country less so because the discoverer's name is on the unit, not the
map · C United States — Edison's fluoroscope and the first clinical X-rays in America came within weeks, so many assume
it began there.
