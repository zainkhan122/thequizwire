## Why this, why now
Half the feed credits Pasteur, a quarter says China. Edward Jenner, a country doctor in Berkeley, Gloucestershire, gave the
first vaccine on 14 May 1796 — cowpox from milkmaid Sarah Nelmes's hand into 8-year-old James Phipps — then challenged the boy
with smallpox and nothing happened (Riedel 2005; Britannica; Jenner Museum). Smallpox killed about 3 in 10 of its victims; the
WHO declared it eradicated in 1980, the only human disease ever wiped out. Body & Brain #7 and #8 were country tables;
this is a village, a date and a line to Geneva.
