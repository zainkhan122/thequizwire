# Where was the FIRST marathon race run?

## Hook fact
Greece, 1896: Marathon to Athens, about 40 km — and the distance we run today was not set there but in London, 12 years later.

## Show it
On the reveal the course draws itself from Marathon down the coast into the Panathenaic Stadium and a runner sticker rides the line as it draws (keep: it IS the answer).
Then a single winner's card — 2:58:50, a water carrier — with a runner sticker crashing in beside the stadium pin.
Then the twist shown as two blocks side by side: Athens 1896 ~40 km against London 1908 42.195 km; the taller block is the
one everyone runs now. Last, a year counter rolls 1921 → 2026 under the words "same distance" — a century of nobody touching it.

## Beat order
1. Winner card + sticker (a person, before any more numbers) → 2. Compare blocks (the surprise: the distance moved) →
3. Counter 1921→2026 (how long it has held). Human → surprise → scale of time.

## Different from
Sports #12 (Brazil) is a bar race → appearance counter → sad mascot at a pin. Here there is no bar race and no character beat:
route reveal → card+sticker → compare → year counter.

## Swipe test
8 s: the course is drawing itself along the coast toward Athens.
15 s: the runner sticker slams in next to the stadium with 2:58:50.
23 s: two blocks rise and the second one is taller — "wait, the distance changed?"

## Devices
route + travel (runner rides the course on the reveal), card, sticker, pin, compare, counter, cam

## Media
`File:1896 Olympic marathon.jpg` (Burton Holmes, 1901, public domain) — the actual runners entering the Panathenaic Stadium in
1896. It rises into the frame in beat 1 while the voice says "a Greek water carrier named Louis won it": the viewer sees the
real race, not an icon of one. One photo only; the course route and the runner sticker already carry the reveal, and the
compare block carries the twist — a second photo would compete with the numbers.

## Pace (P3, 2026-10-21)

UK flag-ball on "London" → castle pops on "Windsor"; s07 years run on "official" → counter gives way to a THEN / NOW split on
"changed". Measured ≈ 16–18 events / 10 s (CI voice timing) with no static span (reelkit/pacelint.py).
