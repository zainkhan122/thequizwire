# Where is the DEEPEST lake on Earth?

## Signal
suggest: "deepest lake in the world" (YouTube autocomplete, "deepest / biggest / oldest lake" family in review/signals.md);
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Baikal; our stats (dispatch/stats.md): Geo Blitz superlatives with a
counted number (#20 coldest capital, #26 longest coastline) retain through the reveal; this one adds a comparison that
reframes the number.

## Why this, why now
Most viewers guess the USA (Great Lakes) or Africa (Tanganyika). Lake Baikal in Siberia is 1,642 m deep and holds about a
fifth of the planet's unfrozen fresh water — more than all five Great Lakes combined (Britannica; UNESCO). 336 rivers flow
in and only one, the Angara, flows out: a route the map can chase.

## Viewer takeaway
"The deepest lake is Baikal, Russia — 1,642 m, about a fifth of all unfrozen fresh water, more than the Great Lakes together."
