# Brief — Sports Sprint #14 · How many men's Cricket World Cups has Australia won?

## Hook fact
Six — 1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015, 2023 — as many as every other winner's top three combined, including a 34-match World Cup unbeaten run (1999–2011) and the 2023 final in Ahmedabad in front of 92,453 people.

## Show it
A chart-first Short. Mystery zoom-out opener over the Indian Ocean (the question is a number, so the map stays coy). Reveal = Australia's flag lifted into the continent with a 6 TITLES card listing the years. Then the entire winners' table as a bar chart on the map (Australia towering over India, West Indies, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, England), a counter racing to 34 for the unbeaten run while Australia pulses, and a flight to India: the country fills, the Ahmedabad pin drops, the crowd counter spins to 92,453.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — flag-lift Australia, tag, 6 TITLES card with all six years.
2. s05 bars — winners' table staggers in as the voice names the other winners.
3. s06 counter — 0 → 34 "World Cup matches unbeaten", Australia pulses on "defeat".
4. s07 flight — camera to India, second highlight, Ahmedabad pin, 2023-final tag, crowd counter 0 → 92,453, wow flag-ball.

## Different from
Sports #12 (Brazil, football) used three bars + an appearances counter on a single country; #13 (Marathon) a route and a distance split. This is the first number-answer question on the channel, the first six-row chart, and the first explanation that leaves the answer country for the venue of its latest win.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — satellite stage already drifting; options are three numbers, which is new for the channel.
- 5 s — the timer runs over an empty ocean: no country shown, so the number must be guessed, not read off the map.
- 14 s — the bar chart drops row by row; Australia's bar dwarfs the rest — the "oh" moment.
