## Why this, why now
Everyone assumes the USA. It was Cape Town, 1967, Christiaan Barnard at Groote Schuur — and the patient lived 18 days. A
Body & Brain Short that is still a map story: three countries, a hospital pin, a real photograph of the operating team, and a
counter for the 18 days. The medical fact (why a transplanted heart beats without nerves) is the hook-after-the-reveal.

## Viewer takeaway
"The first heart transplant wasn't in America — and a transplanted heart beats with no nerves attached."

## Question shape
which-country-first (place of a first) — framed as "happened in which country" so the stem is new ("the first") and the
sentence lands on the map. Options are the three places anyone would guess.

## Options and why they fool

— strong transplant history (first UK heart transplant 1968) and a plausible "first".
