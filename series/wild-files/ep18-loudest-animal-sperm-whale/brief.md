# Brief — Wild Files #18 · What is the LOUDEST animal on Earth?

## Hook fact
Sperm whale echolocation clicks have been measured at source levels up to 236 dB re 1 µPa underwater, the most intense
biological sound recorded (Møhl et al. 2003, JASA; recorded off Andenes, Norway). Blue whale calls reach 188–191 dB
underwater (Frontiers in Marine Science 2021). Howler monkeys, the loudest land animals, reach about 140 dB in air and carry
about 5 km (Nat Geo; Current Biology 2015). Each click lasts about 100 µs and is produced in the spermaceti organ; sperm
whales hunt squid beyond 2,000 m by echolocation (NOAA).
