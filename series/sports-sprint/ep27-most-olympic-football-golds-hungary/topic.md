## Why this, why now
Brazil and Argentina have 2 golds each. Hungary has 3 (1952, 1964, 1968), a record it shares with Great Britain
(1900, 1908, 1912) but holds as the most recent (olympics.com). The 1952 side were the Magical Magyars of Puskás, who
went 42 matches unbeaten from 1950 to the 1954 World Cup final (FIFA; Britannica). The twist: Hungary has not
qualified for the Olympic men's tournament since 1972 — the record is held by a team nobody under 60 has seen play.

qualified since 1972."
