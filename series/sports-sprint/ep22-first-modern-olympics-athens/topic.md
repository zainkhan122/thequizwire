## Why this, why now
Everyone knows "Olympics = Greece" but almost nobody can say when, how small or how male the first Games were: 241 athletes,
14 nations, no women (IOC). The stadium was rebuilt in marble for the occasion; the first ever marathon ran 40 km from
Marathon into it and a water carrier won (Britannica). Rated EASY on purpose — the engagement is in the details, not the
answer.
