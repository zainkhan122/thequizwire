## Why this, why now
People assume a Greek runner opened the modern Games. The first title decided in Athens, on 6 April 1896, was the triple jump,
won by James Connolly, a Boston student who was refused leave by Harvard, quit, and crossed the Atlantic on a freighter. The
jump (13.71 m) is longer than a London bus; the gap since the last ancient champion was about 1,500 years. A person-and-moment
story, not a tally — different from #16's count of host cities and #11's host city.
