# Brief — Wild Files #13 · Where do MOST of the world's wild lions live?

## Hook fact
Tanzania: about 17,200 lions in the 2024/25 national census — more than every other country combined. Africa has only 22–25 thousand left, lions have vanished from 94 % of their range, and the only wild lions outside Africa are 891 Asiatic lions in Gir, India.

## Show it
An East-Africa satellite stage; the camera zooms out from a tight Tanzania under the question (mystery-zoom-out) so the answer is not readable before the reveal. Reveal = Tanzania fills lion-gold with a pulse, "Tanzania" tag, census card. Then South Africa and Kenya light dim with their counts as they are named and the continent counter climbs to 23,000 with a lion sticker. Wide on the continent: the range counter runs to 94 % and the sad flag-ball pops. Last beat flies across the Arabian Sea: India lights, the Gir tag lands, the ASIATIC LIONS card, the lion sticker throbs.

## Different from
Wild Files #11 (elephants) was a Botswana counter with a photo rise; #12 (polar bears) a true/false hemisphere split. This one ends in a different continent, uses a percent counter for loss, and has no photo — the counts are the evidence.
