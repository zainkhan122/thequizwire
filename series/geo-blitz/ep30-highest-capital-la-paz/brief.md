# Brief — Geo Blitz #30 · Which country has the HIGHEST capital city?

## Hook fact
La Paz, Bolivia's administrative capital, lies at about 3,640 m — the highest national capital (Britannica); Sucre, the
constitutional capital, at about 2,800 m. Quito about 2,850 m; Kathmandu about 1,400 m (Britannica; Wikipedia list). El
Alto International Airport above La Paz is at 4,061 m. At 3,500 m the available oxygen is roughly two-thirds of sea level
(CDC Yellow Book); Bolivia's home record at altitude led FIFA to attempt a ban on internationals above 2,500 m in 2007.
