## Why this, why now
Everyone says Saudi Arabia (the name) or Russia (the size). Venezuela holds the most proven crude on Earth — about 303 billion
barrels against Saudi Arabia's 267 billion (OPEC 2025) — nearly all of it tar-like heavy crude in the Orinoco Belt along one
river, and the country pumps barely 1 % of the world's oil. A resource question with a built-in twist (holds the most, sells
almost none) — different from #21's count of lakes and #20's temperature.

## Viewer takeaway
"Venezuela sits on the most proven oil in the world — 303 billion barrels in the Orinoco Belt — and pumps barely 1 % of the
world's supply."
