## Why this, why now
People reach for the United States. China is home to about 148 million adults with diabetes — 1 in 4 of every case on Earth —
India is second with about 90 million, the United States has about 38 million. Worldwide it is 589 million people and a large
share do not yet know. The date hook is built in: 14 November is Frederick Banting's birthday, the Toronto doctor who co-discovered
insulin. A where question with a calendar peg — different from #6 (stroke deaths, a per-year tally) and #5 (first transplant).
