## Why this, why now
Everyone says cheetah. The peregrine falcon's hunting dive has been clocked at 389 km/h — more than three times a cheetah or a
motorway car — and the record was measured over Washington State with a falconer's bird diving beside a skydiver. The kicker is
the comeback: DDT nearly wiped the species out in the 1960s; today they nest on skyscrapers from New York to London. A speed
question, not a count — different from #13 (most lions, a tally) and #12 (true-or-false ranges).
