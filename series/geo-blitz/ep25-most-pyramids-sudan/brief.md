# Brief — Geo Blitz #25 · Which country has the MOST pyramids?

## Hook fact
Sudan has more than 200 pyramids, built by the Kingdom of Kush along the Nile, most at Meroë — nearly twice Egypt's ~138
(UNESCO; National Geographic; Smithsonian; Britannica). Kushite kings ruled Egypt as pharaohs from c. 744 BC.

## Show it
Wide-drift opener over the Red Sea under the question; three flag options (eg, mx, sd). Reveal = flash-cut onto Sudan with
the "Sudan · 200+ pyramids" tag and the 200+ PYRAMIDS card. Tighten on Sudan: the Egypt counter runs 0 → 138 on "Egypt"
while the Egyptian flag-ball thinks; a classical-building sticker on "steeper"; the Meroë pin and "Meroe · 200+" tag on
"Meroe". Ease north so both countries share the frame: a crown on "copycat", the 744 BC card on "ruled", Sudan pulses on
"century", the Sudanese flag-ball grins. Pull out to the whole Nile: the Khartoum pin, then a camel sticker rides a drawn
river line from Meroë to Giza until "Giza", where the pin and "Giza · 1600 km" tag land; a wave sticker on "Nile".

## Different from
#24 (time zones) was bars + a split + a second-colour highlight; #23 a ring + counter + split; #22 bars + belt + split;
#21 bars + canoe route. This one has no bars and no split: a counter for the rival's number, then a drawn river journey
with a travelling sticker — the first Geo Blitz to use route + travel since #21, and the first to pin three cities.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — the Red Sea drifting under MOST in yellow; three flags about to land.
- 7 s — options up, timer running; nearly everyone has said Egypt.
- 12 s — a flash and Sudan is lit, the 200+ card lands — not Egypt.
- 19 s — a counter ticks up to 138 under the word Egypt, and stops short.
