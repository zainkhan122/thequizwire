## Why this, why now
The size trap: people pick the blue whale because it is the biggest, but the loudest measured animal sound is a sperm whale
click at 236 dB underwater (Møhl et al. 2003, JASA), against about 188 dB for a blue whale (Frontiers 2021) and 140 dB for a
howler monkey, the loudest on land (Nat Geo; Current Biology 2015). Honest caveat on screen: water and air decibels differ.
The click lasts a fraction of a millisecond and is used to hunt squid 2 km down (NOAA). The record was recorded off Andenes,
Norway — a real place to pin.
