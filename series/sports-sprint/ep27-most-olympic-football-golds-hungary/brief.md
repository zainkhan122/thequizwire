# Brief — Sports Sprint #27 · Who has the MOST Olympic golds in men's football?

## Hook fact
Hungary has 3 Olympic men's football golds — 1952 Helsinki, 1964 Tokyo, 1968 Mexico City — a total matched only by
Great Britain's 1900, 1908 and 1912 titles; Brazil (2016, 2020) and Argentina (2004, 2008) have 2 each (olympics.com;
Britannica). The 1952 team were the Magical Magyars of Puskás, Kocsis and Hidegkuti, unbeaten in 42 consecutive
matches from May 1950 to the 1954 World Cup final (FIFA; Britannica). Their home was the Népstadion in Budapest
(opened 1953, replaced by the Puskás Aréna in 2019). Hungary's last Olympic men's tournament was Munich 1972, where
they took silver (olympics.com).

Sprint whose SHOW set is {compare, route, travel}: the medal table is stacked blocks, and the team's journey to its
