# Brief — Wild Files #16 · Where do the MOST camels live?

## Hook fact
Somalia has more than 7 million camels, the most of any nation, in a country of about 18 million people (FAO; Worldmapper;
World Bank). Sudan ~5 M, Kenya ~3 M, Saudi Arabia under 2 M. Australia holds the most feral camels, about 300,000 (DCCEEW).

## Show it
Mystery-zoom-out opener from the Arabian Sea under the question; three flag options (sa, au, so). Reveal = fill-pulse on
Somalia with the "Somalia · 7 million camels" tag and the 7 MILLION+ card. Pull back over the Horn and Arabia: four bars
build on "Sudan" with Somalia on top and a camel sticker; on "Saudi" a dark plate reading ARABIA? wipes off the screen while
the Saudi flag-ball slumps; Somalia pulses. Drift towards the Indian Ocean: the Australian flag-ball thinks on "Australia",
the 300,000 FERAL card on "feral", a desert sticker on "outback". Push back onto Somalia: a meat sticker on "food" becomes
a money bag on "wealth" and a glass of milk on "milk" with the "Somalia · milk in every town" tag; the Somali flag-ball grins.
