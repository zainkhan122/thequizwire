# Brief — Geo Blitz #22 · Where is the MOST oil in the world?

## Hook fact
Venezuela holds about 303 billion barrels of proven crude — the most of any country (Saudi Arabia 267 billion, OPEC 2025) —
nearly all of it extra-heavy crude in the Orinoco Belt, yet it pumps under 1 million barrels a day, about 1 % of world supply.

## Show it
Split-candidates opener: the camera visits Saudi Arabia, Russia and Venezuela as each is named under the question. Reveal =
Venezuela fills dark with a pulse, "Venezuela" tag, the Caracas pin drops and the 303 BILLION card lands. Pull right out so
Venezuela and Saudi Arabia share one frame: Saudi lights dim with "Saudi Arabia · 2nd" and a four-row bar chart grows —
Venezuela, Saudi Arabia, Iran, Russia — the bars make the argument, the voice reads one number; an oil-drum sticker, a shrug on
"Russia". Push deep into eastern Venezuela: from "Orinoco" the belt line draws itself east along the river, the "Orinoco Belt" tag
on "strip", a tar-drop sticker and a pulse on "river". Settle back on the country: a HOLDS / PUMPS split slides in — 303 BILLION
barrels in the ground against 1 % of the world's daily output — the pump sticker, the thinking flag-ball.

## Different from
#21 (lakes) used bars then a canoe travelling a route; #20 (coldest capital) had two counters and a city-vs-rest split; #19 was
bars against a freezer. This one combines bars with a drawn belt (no travelling sticker) and a holds-vs-pumps split — show-set
{bars, route, split} differs from every episode in the last 12 — and the mood is a twist (richest reserve, almost no output)
rather than a record.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — the camera hopping between three candidate countries under MOST in yellow.
- 7 s — flags up, timer running; most viewers have said Saudi Arabia.
- 15 s — four bars grow and the top one is not the one people expected — the picture that makes the answer land.
- 26 s — a HOLDS / PUMPS split slides in from both sides: 303 billion against 1 %.
