# Brief — Geo Blitz #18 · Which of these joined the United Nations MOST recently?

## Hook fact
The newest UN member, South Sudan, joined on 14 July 2011 — five days after independence — and the "oldest-looking" option, Switzerland, only joined in 2002 after a referendum. Two surprises in one triple.

## Show it
Three candidates on one Mercator stage framed Europe-to-East-Africa (bbox −2…42 / −2…52) so Switzerland, Montenegro and South Sudan are all on screen; the camera visits each as it is named. Reveal = South Sudan's flag lifted into the country shape, "South Sudan" tag, Juba pin. Then the two distractors light up one by one (dim highlight layer `others`), each with its own year card. Finish wide with the member counter 51 → 193 and the real Independence Day photograph (US State Dept, public domain) rising out of South Sudan.

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — flag-lift South Sudan, tag, MEMBER 193 card, Juba pin on "independence".
2. s05 Montenegro — camera north, country fills, Podgorica pin, "2006 · 28 June 2006" card on "vote".
3. s06 Switzerland — camera west, country fills, Bern pin, flash on "only", ballot-box sticker on "referendum", "2002" card.
4. s07 wide — counter 51 → 193 "UN member states", hidden before "since"; photo rises from South Sudan; wow flag-ball.

## Different from
Ep16 (longest border) used a route line and a bar chart; ep17 (Baikal) used depth bars + photo; ep10/12 used pin spreads. Nothing in the backlog has a second-highlight sequence over three countries plus a counter plus a rise — and this is the first split-candidates opener with a flag-lift reveal in Geo Blitz.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — question card lands while the satellite stage already drifts across the Mediterranean (no bare edges).
- 6 s — camera darts to each candidate as it is read; the third jump lands on South Sudan with 3 s of silence.
- 17 s — Switzerland fills and Switzerland pulses on "only joined in 2002" — the fact most viewers get wrong.

## Devices
candidate-hop opener (geo.cam_to per candidate) · geo.highlight_in with flag lift (answer) · geo.highlight_in on a second dim layer for Montenegro / Switzerland · geo.pin_in ×3 · fact cards ×4 · geo.highlight_pulse · sticker 🗳️ · chart.counter_run 51→193 · geo.rise photo (PD, Commons) · flag-ball characters ss/ch + wow reaction.
