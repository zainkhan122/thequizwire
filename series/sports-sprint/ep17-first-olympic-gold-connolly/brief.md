# Brief — Sports Sprint #17 · Who won the FIRST gold medal of the modern Olympics?

## Hook fact
The first champion of the modern Olympics was James Connolly of the United States — triple jump, Panathenaic Stadium, Athens,
6 April 1896, 13.71 m — a Harvard student who was refused leave, withdrew, and sailed to Europe on a freighter.

## Show it
Wide-drift opener over the North Atlantic under the question, three flags over their countries. Reveal = the US flag lifts into
the country's shape, "United States" tag, then the camera jumps to Athens where the stadium pin drops and the 6 APRIL 1896 card
lands (the "Athens · 1896" tag follows at s05 so it never sits in the dead zone of the reveal camera). Hold on Athens while a
two-block compare rises: Connolly's 13.71 m beside an 11.23 m London bus — the jump is seen, not told; medal sticker, bus
sticker, Greece dims as the host. Pull out to the whole ocean: Boston pin and tag on "Harvard", the sad flag-ball on "quit", and
from "ship" the freighter route draws east from Boston through Gibraltar to Athens with a ship sticker at the start. Back in on
Athens: the 1,500 YEARS card on "crowned", a laurel sticker, a last pulse and the happy flag-ball on "dropout".

## Beat order
1. s04 reveal — flag-lift United States, tag; stadium pin + 6 APRIL 1896 card (Athens tag lands at s05).
2. s05 the jump — chart.compare_in Connolly 13.71 m vs London bus 11.23 m; 🥇 on "metres", 🚌 + Greece dim on "bus".
3. s06 the journey — Boston pin/tag on "Harvard", sad flag-ball on "quit", route_draw Boston → Athens from "ship", US pulse.
4. s07 the gap — 1,500 YEARS card on "crowned", 🏅 on "years", pulse + happy flag-ball on "dropout".

## Different from
#16 (most Games hosted) was a counter and an erase of the losing guess; #15 (Davis Cup) drew a ship route with a travelling
sticker and bars; #13 (marathon) used a then-vs-now split. This one leads with a compare chart (first in Sports Sprint), draws a
route without a travelling sticker, and ends on a time-gap card — show-set {compare, route} differs from every episode of the
last 12, and the reveal is a flag lift, not a fill.

## Swipe test
- 1 s — the Atlantic drifting under FIRST in yellow; three flags about to land.
- 7 s — flags up, timer running; most viewers have said Greece.
- 14 s — two blocks rise and the jump out-reaches a bus — the picture that makes the answer land.
- 24 s — a line draws itself across the whole ocean while the voice says he quit college.
