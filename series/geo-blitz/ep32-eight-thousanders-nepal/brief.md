# Brief — Geo Blitz #32 · How many peaks above eight thousand metres stand in NEPAL?

## Hook fact
Nepal contains or shares eight of the fourteen peaks above 8,000 m: Everest, Kangchenjunga, Lhotse, Makalu, Cho Oyu,
Dhaulagiri, Manaslu and Annapurna (Nepal Department of Tourism; Britannica "Himalayas"). Pakistan has five including
K2; China five, four of them shared with Nepal (Wikipedia "Eight-thousander"; Britannica "K2"). Everest, Lhotse, Makalu
and Cho Oyu stand on the China border, Kangchenjunga on the India border; Dhaulagiri, Manaslu and Annapurna rise
entirely inside Nepal. Everest's height, 8,848.86 m, was announced jointly by Nepal and China on 8 December 2020
(Survey Department of Nepal; BBC).
