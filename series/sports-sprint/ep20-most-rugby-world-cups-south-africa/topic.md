# Which nation has the MOST Rugby World Cups?

## Signal
suggest: "most rugby world cups" / "rugby world cup 2027" (YouTube autocomplete, checked 2026-11-07; the 2027 tournament in
Australia is eleven months away and the draw coverage has started); wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_World_Cup;
our stats (dispatch/stats.md): Sports Sprint's "most World Cups" football episode (#12 Brazil) is still the series' best
first-24 h; the rugby equivalent has the same shape with a less obvious answer.

## Why this, why now
Most viewers answer New Zealand: the All Blacks are the most feared team in the sport. South Africa holds the record — four
titles (1995, 2007, 2019, 2023) to New Zealand's three (World Rugby; Britannica), the last won 12–11 over the All Blacks in
Paris (BBC Sport). The story beat: the first came at home in 1995, a year after apartheid ended, with Mandela in a Springbok
jersey. Rugby World Cup 2027 is the news hook.

## Viewer takeaway
"South Africa has the most Rugby World Cups — 4; New Zealand 3; the 2023 final was 12–11; the first was Johannesburg 1995."
