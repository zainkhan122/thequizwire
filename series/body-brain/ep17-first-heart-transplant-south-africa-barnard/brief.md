# Brief — Body & Brain #17 · Which country did the FIRST human heart transplant?

## Hook fact
Christiaan Barnard performed the first human-to-human heart transplant at Groote Schuur Hospital, Cape Town, South
Africa, in the early hours of Sunday 3 December 1967; the patient, Louis Washkansky, lived 18 days (Britannica
"Christiaan Barnard"; Heart of Cape Town Museum). Norman Shumway and Richard Lower had developed the technique at
Stanford in hundreds of dog experiments; Shumway's first US adult transplant followed on 6 January 1968 (NLM/PMC).
Median survival after adult heart transplant is now about 12–13 years (ISHLT registry). Roughly 6,000 or more heart
transplants are performed worldwide each year (ISHLT; GODT/WHO).
