# Brief — Sports Sprint #26 · Which country hosted the FIRST Winter Olympics?

## Hook fact
The first Olympic Winter Games were held at Chamonix, France, 25 January–5 February 1924, at the foot of Mont Blanc.
They were staged as an "International Winter Sports Week" attached to Paris 1924 and designated the first Olympic
Winter Games by the IOC retroactively in 1926 (olympics.com "Chamonix 1924"; Britannica). 16 nations and 258 athletes
(247 men, 11 women) competed in 16 events (olympics.com). Norway topped the table with 17 medals, Finland took 11, and
the host France won 3, all bronze (Wikipedia medal table; olympics.com). Switzerland hosted the second Games at St.
Moritz in 1928 (olympics.com).
