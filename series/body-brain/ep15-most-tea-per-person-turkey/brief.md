# Brief — Body & Brain #15 · Who drinks the MOST tea per person?

## Hook fact
Turkey drinks about 3.2 kg of tea per person a year, the most in the world; Ireland follows at about 2.2 kg and the
United Kingdom at about 1.9 kg; China, the biggest producer and total consumer, is well under 1 kg per head
(Euromonitor via Statista; FAO; Wikipedia list). Turkey grows nearly all its own tea in Rize and neighbouring provinces
on the steep, rainy eastern Black Sea coast (ÇAYKUR; TurkStat; Britannica "Rize"). Black tea is brewed in a stacked
double kettle and served in small tulip-shaped glasses many times a day (Wikipedia "Turkish tea"; Britannica "Tea").
